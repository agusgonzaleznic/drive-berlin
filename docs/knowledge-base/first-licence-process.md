# Getting a first German licence (*Ersterteilung*) — for comparison

> **You almost certainly do not need this route.** *Ersterteilung* ("first grant" of a driving
> licence) is the process for someone who has never held a licence anywhere. You hold an Argentine
> licence, so your route is the *Umschreibung* (conversion) of that licence, filed as a
> third-country conversion. This page is recorded for two reasons: (1) the exam machinery is
> largely shared between the two routes, so the sections on DEKRA, the *Prüfauftrag* and the
> 12-month deadlines are directly useful to you; and (2) if the conversion route closes — most
> obviously if your Argentine licence expires before you file, or if the authority refuses to
> accept it — *Ersterteilung* is the fallback, and it is worth knowing what it costs and how long
> it takes before that becomes urgent.

**Currency of this page.** All facts below come from a research pass dated 3 August 2026 and an
adversarial verification pass dated 4 August 2026. Fees, backlogs and the reform timetable move;
re-check anything marked as a live figure before relying on it.

## Bottom line

- The competent authority is the *Fahrerlaubnisbehörde* (driving licence authority) of Berlin's
  **LABO**, but a first-licence application is normally filed at **any Berlin *Bürgeramt*** (citizens'
  office, 185 of them are listed as competent) or **digitally by a participating Berlin driving school**
  via *Führerschein Antrag Online* (FAO). LABO itself is also a valid filing point. There is no
  self-service online route for individuals ([service 121627](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/121627/)).
- **The theory exam can be sat in Italian** — LABO lists 13 exam languages including Italian,
  Spanish and English. This is the single most useful fact on this page for you, and it applies to
  the conversion route too, since you must pass both German exams.
- **Berlin fee: 45.90 EUR** for the *Ersterteilung* application, plus **6.32 EUR** optional direct
  dispatch of the finished card. **Exam fees: 24.99 EUR** theory and **129.83 EUR** practical
  Klasse B, both including VAT ([service 121627](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/121627/);
  [GebOSt tariff](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stgebo_2011/anlage.html)).
- **Two hard 12-month deadlines** govern both routes: theory must be passed within 12 months of the
  *Prüfauftrag* (exam order) reaching DEKRA, and the practical within 12 months of passing theory
  (*FeV* § 22 Abs. 5). LABO grants **no extensions** — a lapsed authorisation means a completely new
  application and new fees.
- Real Berlin waiting times as of **28 July 2026**: LABO was working on *Ersterteilung* applications
  filed **2 June 2026** (roughly eight weeks) and third-country *Umschreibung* applications filed
  **9 June 2026** ([Bearbeitungsstände](https://www.berlin.de/labo/mobilitaet/aktuelles/aktuelle-bearbeitungsstaende-736453.php)).
- A major German training reform was **approved by cabinet on 20 May 2026** and is targeted to enter
  into force in **early 2027**, subject to parliament. It would abolish compulsory classroom theory,
  shorten the practical exam to 25 minutes and make every theory question worth one point —
  dissolving the current "two 5-point questions wrong = fail" rule
  ([BMV 043/2026](https://www.bmv.de/SharedDocs/DE/Pressemitteilungen/2026/043-schnieder-bezahlbarer-fuehrerschein.html)).

## 1. The authority and where you actually file

The decision-making body is the *Fahrerlaubnisbehörde* of the *Landesamt für Bürger- und
Ordnungsangelegenheiten* (State Office for Citizens' and Public Order Affairs, LABO),
Puttkamerstr. 16–18, 10969 Berlin (Kreuzberg). Telephone (030) 90269-2300 or the city-wide 115.
Opening hours are Monday to Thursday 07:00–14:00 and Friday 07:00–12:00, and access is
appointment-only — the location page states *"Nur für Terminkunden"* ("appointment customers only").
Appointments there are bookable only by telephone or via the contact form, not through the online
booking system. Payment on site is accepted in cash, girocard, VISA, Mastercard, Maestro and Vpay
([service.berlin.de standort 121646](https://service.berlin.de/standort/121646/)).

An earlier draft of this research claimed that first-time applicants never need to visit LABO. That
is **wrong** and was corrected in verification: the LABO *Fahrerlaubnisbehörde* is itself listed
under *"Für Sie zuständig"* ("responsible for you") on the *Ersterteilung* service page, appearing
after the 185 *Bürgeramt* entries. So there are three filing routes:

| Route | Who can use it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Any Berlin *Bürgeramt*, in person | Anyone | *"Persönliche Vorsprache ist erforderlich"* — personal appearance is required. City-wide appointment booking. Bring a paper photo; none are printed on site. |
| *Führerschein Antrag Online* (FAO), filed by your driving school | Only via a participating **Berlin** driving school | Covers *Ersterteilung*, *Erweiterung* (adding a class), BF17 and AM15 — verbatim from the FAO page. Explicitly **not** *Umtausch* or *Umschreibung*. |
| LABO *Fahrerlaubnisbehörde* directly | Anyone, by appointment | Appointment by phone or contact form only. |

Two things are *Bürgeramt*-exclusive rather than LABO-exclusive: a *Führungszeugnis* (certificate of
good conduct) can be co-applied for **only** at a *Bürgeramt* — LABO's own page says *"in der
Fahrerlaubnisbehörde ist das nicht möglich"* — and that certificate is needed only for the D bus
classes anyway.

If your driving school files via FAO it **may not charge you a surcharge**. The LABO FAQ is explicit:

> Darf eine zusätzliche Gebühr für die Online-Antragstellung über die Fahrschule erhoben werden?
> Nein. Für die Antragstellung darf die Fahrschule nur den Betrag berechnen, der auch bei
> Antragstellung im Bürgeramt anfällt.

("May an additional fee be charged for the online application through the driving school? No. For
the application the driving school may only charge the amount that also arises when applying at the
*Bürgeramt*.")

> ⚠️ **Unresolved contradiction relevant to your route.** The verification file states in one place
> that *Umschreibung* is not available through FAO and is a *Bürgeramt* matter, and in another place
> that LABO handles *Umschreibungen* exclusively. Both cannot be right. Neither statement is quoted
> verbatim from a source, so treat the filing point for your conversion as **unverified here** and
> confirm it against the conversion documents in this knowledge base or by calling 115 before you
> book anything.

## 2. The chronological process

The verified sequence for a 2026 *Ersterteilung* in Berlin is:

1. **Enrol with a *Fahrschule*** (driving school). This realistically comes first, because the
   application form demands the name and address of both the driving school **and** the
   *Prüfstelle* (testing centre).
2. **Get the *Sehtest*** (vision test, valid two years) **and the *Erste-Hilfe-Schulung***
   (first-aid course), and obtain a biometric photo on paper.
3. **File the application** at a *Bürgeramt*, at LABO, or via your school through FAO, and pay
   45.90 EUR. The earliest possible filing is six months before you reach the minimum age — a
   constraint that only matters to under-18s.
4. **LABO processes the application**, obtains register extracts, and if it finds no fitness
   concerns issues the *Prüfauftrag* to DEKRA and sends you the *Prüfungszulassung* (exam
   authorisation).
5. **Theory training, then the theory exam** at DEKRA.
6. **Practical training, then the practical exam**, booked exclusively through your driving school.
7. **On passing**, the examiner hands you a *vorläufiger Nachweis der Fahrerlaubnis* (provisional
   proof of driving entitlement, VNF), and the plastic card arrives by post in roughly ten working
   days.

Two process facts that the original research omitted and that verification flagged as materially
important:

- **The practical exam must be taken at your main residence or at your place of school, vocational
  training, study or work**, and the supporting evidence must reach the *Fahrerlaubnisbehörde*
  *before* the *Prüfauftrag* is handed over: *"Entsprechende Nachweise sind vor der Abgabe des
  Prüfauftrages bei der Fahrerlaubnisbehörde einzureichen."* Missing this window cannot be fixed
  afterwards, because the *Prüfstelle* can no longer be changed once the *Prüfungszulassung* has
  issued. For a Berlin resident testing in Berlin this is automatic, but it is a real trap
  for anyone who lives in one city and works in another.
- **For under-age applicants the *Prüfungszulassung* letter is only dispatched three months before
  the minimum age is reached.** Applies to 17-year-olds, not to you.

## 3. Documents

The following list is confirmed verbatim from the official *Ersterteilung* service page for Klasse B.

| Document | Requirement |
|---|---|
| *Personalausweis* or passport | As an Italian citizen this is the **only** identity document needed. No residence permit, no *Meldebescheinigung* (registration certificate) on the list. |
| Biometric passport photo | *"1 aktuelles, biometrisches Passfoto auf Papier"* — one current biometric photo **on paper**. *"Vor Ort werden keine Papierfotos gedruckt"*: no photos are printed on site. |
| Name and address of the *Fahrschule* | Mandatory field. |
| Name and address of the *Prüfstelle* | Mandatory field. |
| *Erste-Hilfe-Schulung* certificate | Full first-aid course. *FeV* § 19 requires *"mindestens neun Unterrichtseinheiten zu je 45 Minuten"* — at least nine units of 45 minutes, i.e. a floor, not a fixed figure. The older *"Sofortmaßnahmen am Unfallort"* course is explicitly **not** accepted: *"Unterweisungen über die „Sofortmaßnahmen am Unfallort“ finden keine Anerkennung."* Exemptions for doctors, nurses, paramedics and similar under *FeV* § 19 Abs. 3. |
| *Sehtestbescheinigung* | *"Nicht älter als 2 Jahre"* — no older than two years; required for classes AM, A1, A2, A, B, BE, L and T. |

A formal driving-school training contract is not on the document list, even though the school's
details are mandatory.

**Prerequisite, not a document:** *"Hauptwohnsitz in Berlin"* — main residence in Berlin. This is
checked against the population register rather than proved by you; *FeV* § 22 Abs. 1 authorises the
authority to obtain register information (*"durch Einholung von Auskünften aus dem Melderegister"*).

**Two corrections to the original research**, both concerning documents that apply to other
personas:

- A *Führungszeugnis* is required **only for classes D, D1, DE and D1E** (buses) — *not* for the C
  lorry classes, as the research claimed. It costs 13.00 EUR (*Belegart* "O") and can be
  co-applied for only at a *Bürgeramt*.
- The *Funktions- und Leistungstest* is likewise a **D-class-only** requirement. Only the vision
  examination (two years) and the certificate of physical and mental fitness (one year) apply to
  both C and D classes.

**Useful detail from the LABO FAQ:** *"Die Ausbildung in 1. Hilfe hat kein Ablaufdatum"* — the
first-aid certificate never expires. Only the *Sehtest* has a shelf life. LABO does warn, however,
that a certificate submitted with an earlier application may no longer be on file.

## 6. The *Prüfauftrag* — the mechanism that matters to both routes

The *Prüfauftrag* (exam order) is the instrument by which the authority commissions the *Technische
Prüfstelle* (technical testing centre) to examine you. *FeV* § 22 Abs. 4 — the section is headed
*"Verfahren bei der Behörde und der Technischen Prüfstelle"* — provides:

> …hat die Fahrerlaubnisbehörde die zuständige Technische Prüfstelle für den Kraftfahrzeugverkehr
> mit der Prüfung zu beauftragen und ihr den vorbereiteten Führerschein (§ 25) ohne Angabe des
> Datums der Erteilung der beantragten Klasse unmittelbar zu übersenden.

("…the driving licence authority shall commission the competent technical testing centre for motor
vehicle traffic with the examination and shall send it directly the prepared licence (§ 25) without
stating the date of grant of the applied-for class.")

So your plastic card is manufactured **before** you sit the exams and sits at DEKRA waiting for a
date to be stamped on it. You separately receive the *Prüfungszulassung* (exam authorisation).

Two deadlines then run, both in *FeV* § 22 Abs. 5:

| Deadline | Runs from | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Pass the theory exam | Arrival of the *Prüfauftrag* at the *Prüfstelle* | 12 months |
| Pass the practical exam | Passing the theory exam | 12 months |

LABO restates them in plain language: *"Die theoretische Prüfung muss innerhalb von 12 Monaten nach
der Zulassung zur Prüfung bestanden werden. Nach dem Bestehen der theoretischen Prüfung muss die
praktische Prüfung innerhalb von 12 Monaten bestanden werden."* Note the **base measures differ
slightly**: the regulation counts from the *Prüfauftrag* arriving at DEKRA, LABO counts from your
authorisation to sit the exam. Assume the earlier of the two and leave margin.

**No extensions.** The LABO FAQ is blunt: *"Meine Prüfzulassung ist abgelaufen, was muss ich tun? –
Bitte stellen Sie einen neuen Antrag im Bürgeramt."* ("My exam authorisation has expired, what must
I do? – Please file a new application at the *Bürgeramt*.") And: *"Fristverlängerungen werden nicht
gewährt."* A lapsed authorisation means a fresh application and fresh fees.

One precision point: § 22 Abs. 5 is worded as a *soll* provision (*"Die Technische Prüfstelle SOLL
den Prüfauftrag … zurückgeben"* — the testing centre *shall/should* return the order), so avoid
saying the authorisation lapses automatically by operation of law. In practice LABO treats it as
expired.

**After the *Prüfungszulassung* issues, the *Prüfstelle* cannot be changed.** Combined with the rule
that the practical must be taken where you live or work, and that the evidence for that must arrive
*before* the *Prüfauftrag* is handed over, this is the one irreversible decision point in the
process.

## 7. Validity windows, exam content and retakes

### Windows

| Rule | Provision | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Theory exam no earlier than three months before the minimum age | *FeV* § 16 | *"Sie darf frühestens drei Monate vor Erreichen des Mindestalters abgenommen werden"* |
| Training completion no more than two years old at the theory exam | *FeV* § 16 | *"Der Abschluss der Ausbildung darf nicht länger als zwei Jahre zurückliegen"* |
| Practical only after passing theory, and no earlier than one month before the minimum age | *FeV* § 17 | *"frühestens einen Monat vor Erreichen des Mindestalters"* |
| The two 12-month windows | *FeV* § 22 Abs. 5 | See section 6 |

The age-linked windows are only binding on applicants approaching 18; for an adult they are
satisfied from the outset.

### Klasse B exam content (*FeV* Anlage 7)

- **Theory:** 30 questions, 110 points available, a maximum of **10 error points** permitted. You
  also fail if you get **two 5-point questions wrong** (*"zwei Fragen mit Wertigkeit 5 falsch
  beantwortet"*).
- **Practical:** **55 minutes** in total, of which at least **30 minutes** must be pure driving time.

Both of these are scheduled to change — see section 11.

### Retakes

**Correction to the original research on two counts.** The 14-day wait is **statutory**, not merely
administrative practice. *FeV* § 18 provides:

> Eine nicht bestandene Prüfung darf nicht vor Ablauf eines angemessenen Zeitraums, in der Regel
> mindestens zwei Wochen, wiederholt werden.

("A failed exam may not be repeated before an appropriate period has elapsed, as a rule at least two
weeks.") Cite *FeV* § 18 for this, not the secondary driving-portal sources the research relied on.

And the penalty for cheating is far harsher than reported: on a *Täuschungshandlung* (act of
deception), *FeV* § 18 allows the retake deadline to be set at **up to nine months**
(*"kann die Frist für die Wiederholung der Prüfung auf bis zu neun Monate festgelegt werden"*), not
the "around six weeks" the research stated.

> ⚠️ **Unverified:** that retakes are **unlimited** in number. This rests on secondary sources
> (fuehrerscheine.de and similar) and was not confirmed against primary law in the verification
> pass. What *is* certain is that each attempt costs the DEKRA fee again plus your driving school's
> presentation fee, and that a failed theory exam must be repeated in full.

## 9. *Probezeit* — the two-year probationary period

*StVG* § 2a Abs. 1 provides: *"die Probezeit dauert zwei Jahre vom Zeitpunkt der Erteilung an"* —
the probationary period lasts two years from the moment of grant. The escalation ladder in
§ 2a Abs. 2 Nr. 1–3 is:

1. One serious (**A**) offence or two less serious (**B**) offences → mandatory *Aufbauseminar*
   (advanced training seminar), **plus** a two-year extension of the *Probezeit* under § 2a Abs. 2a:
   *"Die Probezeit verlängert sich um zwei Jahre, wenn die Teilnahme an einem Aufbauseminar … angeordnet worden ist."*
2. Ignoring the seminar order → the licence is revoked.
3. Further offences → written warning plus a recommendation of *verkehrspsychologische Beratung*
   (traffic-psychology counselling) within two months, then revocation.

**The 0.00 rule.** *StVG* § 24c imposes an absolute prohibition during the *Probezeit* **and** for
all drivers under 21, covering both alcohol and tetrahydrocannabinol since the 2024 cannabis
amendment. There is a verbatim medical exemption: the provision is *"nicht anzuwenden, wenn die
Substanz Tetrahydrocannabinol aus der bestimmungsgemäßen Einnahme eines für einen konkreten
Krankheitsfall verschriebenen Arzneimittels herrührt"* (not applicable where the THC stems from the
proper use of a medicine prescribed for a specific illness).

A § 24c breach is classified as an **A-offence** at law, by *FeV* Anlage 12, Abschnitt A.2.3:
*"Verstöße gegen § 24a oder § 24c des Straßenverkehrsgesetzes (Alkohol, Cannabis, andere berauschende
Mittel)"*. It therefore triggers the seminar and the two-year extension. From 0.3 per mille with
impairment, or 0.5 per mille outright, the general and harsher alcohol offences apply on top.

> ⚠️ **Unverified:** the commonly cited sanction of **250 EUR plus one point** for a § 24c breach.
> The verification pass could not surface a § 24c entry in the accessible *BKatV* annex; the figure
> is supported only by secondary sources such as bussgeldkatalog.org and ADAC. Treat it as
> indicative.

> ⚠️ **Not an official tariff:** the *Aufbauseminar* price of roughly 250–450 EUR is a market
> estimate from providers, not a published fee.

> **Open question for your route.** Whether a *Probezeit* attaches when an Argentine licence is
> converted is **not addressed** in this source material, which documents *Ersterteilung* only. The
> governing provision is *StVG* § 2a Abs. 1, of which the research quotes only the duration
> sentence. Check the conversion documents in this knowledge base rather than assuming either way.

## 10. Getting the card, and the provisional proof

Berlin has operated a "provisional proof first" practice since 1 July 2010. The confirmed mechanics:

- **The card is produced only after all applied-for classes are passed:** *"Der Kartenführerschein
  wird grundsätzlich erst nach Bestehen der Fahrerlaubnisprüfungen für alle beantragten Klassen
  hergestellt."*
- **Delivery** is *"durch die Deutsche Post AG als „Einschreiben Einwurf" an der bei Antragstellung
  angegebenen Anschrift"* — by registered letter dropped in the letterbox, to the address given **at
  application**. Around ten working days per the FAQ. Your name must be on the letterbox.
- **The *vorläufiger Nachweis der Fahrerlaubnis* (VNF)**, handed over by the examiner on the day you
  pass, is *"in Verbindung mit einem amtlichen Lichtbildausweis drei Monate gültig"* — valid three
  months in combination with official photo ID. It is valid **in Germany only**, grounded in
  *FeV* § 22 Abs. 4 and Anlage 8a. Do not plan to drive to Italy on it.
- **Address changes are not synchronised.** *"Anschriftenänderungen müssen der
  Fahrerlaubnisbehörde mitgeteilt werden, da die Meldeanschrift nicht automatisch aktualisiert
  wird."* If you move within Berlin mid-process and only tell the *Bürgeramt*, delivery fails.
- **If a card cannot be delivered**, its return to LABO *"kann mehrere Wochen in Anspruch nehmen"* —
  can take several weeks. LABO asks to be contacted four weeks before your VNF's three-month
  validity runs out.
- **If the VNF lapses while other applied-for classes are still pending**, LABO extends it **free of
  charge**. Separately, on explicit request a chargeable card licence covering only the
  already-passed classes can be produced — useful precisely because the VNF is domestic-only.
- **There is no readiness notification** for collection: *"es gibt keine gesonderte Benachrichtigung
  über die Fertigstellung Ihres Führerscheins."*

federal states including Berlin (also Brandenburg, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Saxony, Saxony-Anhalt and

*"Für die Theorieprüfung brauchen Sie KEINEN Termin, kommen Sie einfach vorbei!"* ("You need NO
appointment for the theory exam, just come by!"). Practical exams are scheduled **exclusively**
through your driving school.

This is the most decision-relevant fact on the page for you, and the original research badly

## 12. Your status as an EU citizen, and the residence rule

The prerequisite is *"Hauptwohnsitz in Berlin"*. If Berlin is only a secondary residence, the
official page says the application *"kann nur in begründeten Ausnahmefällen und mit Zustimmung der
Behörde des Hauptwohnsitzes gestellt werden"* — only in justified exceptional cases and with the
consent of the main-residence authority. As a registered Berlin resident you satisfy this.

*FeV* § 7 defines the *ordentlicher Wohnsitz* (ordinary residence) by the 185-day test. Verification
flagged one carve-out that matters for exactly this reader profile: **a national of another EU or
EEA state who is in Germany solely to attend school or university does not thereby acquire an
ordinary residence** — but the licence is nonetheless issued once the stay has lasted at least six
months. So an Italian *student* in Berlin must be able to show a six-month stay; an Italian who
*works* in Berlin is covered by the ordinary 185-day rule.

**Two provisions that bear directly on someone who already holds a foreign licence.** These matter
to you even on the conversion route, and they matter especially if you ever file an *Ersterteilung*
while holding an Argentine licence:

- ***FeV* § 22 Abs. 2** obliges the authority to obtain *Fahreignungsregister* (driver fitness
  register) and *Zentrales Fahrerlaubnisregister* (central driving licence register) extracts **at
  the applicant's cost**, and permits it to require **foreign register extracts** — usually via the
  *Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt* — plus a *Führungszeugnis* where there are indications of a prior foreign
  licence. Expect to be asked about licences held in Argentina and in Italy, and expect the
  authority to check.
- ***FeV* § 22 Abs. 2a**: no licence may be issued if one was previously withdrawn in another state
  by a court or authority, for as long as the grounds persist.

## 13. Changes in 2025 / 2026, and the reform expected around early 2027

### Fourth EU Driving Licence Directive — already in force

Directive (EU) 2025/2205 of **22 October 2025** was published in the Official Journal on
**5 November 2025** and entered into force on the twentieth day after publication.

> ⚠️ **Date conflict, unresolved.** That calculation gives 25 November 2025; German Wikipedia says
> 26 November 2025. The directive's own absolute deadlines — 26 November 2028 for transposition and
> 26 November 2029 for application — point to 26 November 2025. Verification's recommendation, which
> this document follows: say **"late November 2025"** and do not assert a precise day.

The directive brings EU-wide probationary-period rules and accompanied driving from 17. Transposition
is due by **26 November 2028**, with application from **26 November 2029** — matching the
"roughly three plus one years" framing.

> ⚠️ **Correction to a widely repeated claim.** "EU digital licence by 2030, Germany aiming for end
> of 2026" is **wrong on both halves**. 26 November 2026 is the deadline for the **Commission** to
> adopt the implementing acts specifying mobile driving licences. Member States must then issue the
> mobile licence as the **default** format only **54 months after adoption of the first such act** —
> i.e. around **2031** — though they may do so voluntarily earlier. "By 2030" is a secondary-source
> simplification, and there is **no evidenced German end-2026 target**.

### German *Fahrausbildung* reform — cabinet 20 May 2026, targeted for early 2027

Approved by cabinet on **20 May 2026** and targeted to enter into force in **early 2027**, subject to
passage through the *Bundestag* and *Bundesrat*. Confirmed contents:

| Change | Detail |
|---|---|
| Compulsory classroom theory abolished | Theory lessons may be taken by app or online instead of in person |
| Lay-accompanied practice drives | Permitted **after** the theory exam is passed, with e.g. a parent. **Correction:** the ministry release does **not** describe this as a pilot — it is a substantive provision. |
| Theory question catalogue cut | From *"über 1.100"* questions by about a third. The specific "~840" figure in the research is arithmetic, not a quoted number. |
| Uniform question weighting | Every question is to count **one point**, dissolving the present 110-point / 5-4-3 weighting — and with it the "two 5-point questions wrong = automatic fail" rule |
| *Sonderfahrten* (mandatory special drives) | **Correction:** the fixed quantities (currently 12) are dropped, but they are **not** simply "replaced by competence standards" — a minimum of one night drive, one *Landstraße* (country road) drive and one *Autobahn* drive **remains mandatory** |
| Practical exam shortened | Driving time cut to the EU minimum of **25 minutes**, from the current 55-minute total with 30 minutes' driving |
| Price transparency | Driving-school prices and pass rates to be published online via the *Mobilithek* |

The ministry gives the current average cost of a Klasse B licence as *"rund 3.400 Euro"* — around
3,400 EUR — and the reform's stated purpose is to cut that substantially. Because the *GebOSt*
tariff 402 permits a reduced fee for a shortened exam, the 129.83 EUR practical fee may also fall.

**Until the reform is law, the current rules still apply throughout 2026**: compulsory theory
lessons, 12 *Sonderfahrten*, the 55-minute practical exam and the point-weighted theory exam.

> ⚠️ **Open question:** whether the reform passes the *Bundestag* and *Bundesrat* unchanged and
> really enters into force in early 2027. Do not rely on any 2027 cost or lesson-count claim until
> it is enacted.

### Other 2025/2026 items

- **B197 / *Automatikregelung*** (in force since **1 April 2021**): you may train and sit the
  practical exam on an automatic and still receive an unrestricted Klasse B, provided at least
  10 × 45 minutes of practical training on a manual is documented. Switching mid-application from
  manual to automatic needs no application change and no fee; the reverse costs from 49.00 EUR.
- **Berlin's FAO** digital application route via driving schools is live for *Ersterteilung*,
  *Erweiterung*, BF17 and AM15. ⚠️ Its launch date and the current number of participating schools
  are not documented in the official sources; one commercial site claimed more than 70, LABO gives
  no figure.
- **The *GebOSt* exam amounts** above are current. ⚠️ The claimed 19 December 2025 amendment date is
  unverified.
- ⚠️ **Unverified (secondary only):** the research states the theory question catalogue receives
  routine updates every April and October.
