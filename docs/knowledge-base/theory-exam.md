# The German theory exam (*Theorieprüfung*) for class B

*Written 4 August 2026 for a holder of an Argentine licence, resident in Berlin, converting it (*Umschreibung*). Facts are stamped to that date; the items most likely to go stale are flagged.*

*Two notes on provenance before you start.* First, the source research for this document (`raw/theory-exam.json`) was fact-checked by a separate adversarial pass (`raw/theory-exam-verification.json`), and where the two disagree the verification wins — corrections and downgrades are marked in place. Second, that verification pass was written for a slightly different reader (an Italian citizen taking a **first** German licence), so its framing about who needs an exam at all does not apply to you; the conversion-specific law below is drawn from `raw/ar-legal-core.json`, which read *Anlage 11 FeV* and *§ 31 FeV* directly against the official consolidated text.

## Bottom line

- **You do have to sit this exam.** Argentina is not listed in *Anlage 11 FeV* (the annex of states whose licences are converted without a full examination), so the general rule of *FeV § 31 Abs. 2* applies and both the theory and the practical exam must be passed (*FeV § 15 Abs. 1*).
- **But you do not have to sit through the lessons.** *FeV § 31 Abs. 2* switches off the training rules entirely: *"sind die Vorschriften über die Ausbildung nicht anzuwenden"* — no 12 + 2 *Doppelstunden* (double lessons) of theory, no minimum driving lessons. See [What applies to the conversion route](#what-applies-to-the-conversion-route).
- **Format:** 30 questions, 110 points, computer-based, no time limit. You may collect at most 10 error points — **except** that two wrong 5-point questions fail you even though that is exactly 10 (*Anlage 7 Nr. 1.2 FeV*).
- **All three of your languages are legally available:** *Anlage 7 Nr. 1.3 FeV* lists English, Spanish and Italian among the 12 permitted foreign languages. Audio support is German-only, and the practical exam has no foreign-language provision at all.
- **Fee: EUR 24.99** gross, unchanged since 31 January 2024 (*GebOSt* Nr. 401.1 + 401.3). In Berlin the theory exam is walk-in with no appointment — but DEKRA wants the fee by bank transfer **at least 10 working days in advance**, which is the sharpest practical trap in the whole process.
- **The question catalogue has been frozen since 1 April 2025** and cannot change before 1 October 2026, so any study material advertising the April 2025 catalogue is fully current for an exam taken now.

## What applies to the conversion route

Most of what is written about the German theory exam online is written for an 18-year-old taking a first licence. Here is what you can ignore and what you cannot.

| Topic | Applies to you? |
|---|---|
| The exam itself — 30 questions, 110 points, 10-error-point limit, video and image-selection tasks | **Yes**, in full. The exam an *Umschreibung* candidate sits is the ordinary class B *Ersterteilung* exam. |
| Exam languages, fee, Berlin locations, walk-in procedure, retake rules, 12-month validity | **Yes**, in full. |
| The 12 *Grundstoff* + 2 *Zusatzstoff* mandatory theory *Doppelstunden* (*FahrschAusbO 2012 § 4*) | **No** — waived by *FeV § 31 Abs. 2*. Read the section below anyway, because it explains what the exam assumes you were taught. |
| Mandatory *Sonderfahrten* (special driving lessons: motorway, rural, night) and any minimum number of driving lessons | **No** — same waiver. Voluntary lessons remain strongly advisable, especially for the practical exam. |
| The *Ausbildungsbescheinigung* (training certificate) that ordinary learners hand the examiner | **Probably not** — see [What to bring](#what-to-bring-and-what-happens-on-the-day). This is the weakest link in the analysis and worth one phone call. |
| The "*EUR 3,500–6,000* for a licence" figure quoted by ADAC | **No** — that is a full first licence including ~14 theory lessons and 12 *Sonderfahrten*. Your cost base is the application fee, the two exam fees, and however many voluntary lessons you choose. |
| Pass statistics (44 % class B theory failure in 2025) | **Yes, statistically** — the published rates are not broken down by route, so they describe a population dominated by first-licence learners. Treat them as a warning about the exam's difficulty, not as a prediction for you. |

One asymmetry is worth internalising: the language concession covers the **written theory exam only**. Your Argentine licence, your Italian passport and your Spanish and Italian both help you in the exam room and stop helping you the moment you sit in the car.

## Exam format and scoring

| Item | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | 30 — 20 *Grundstoff* (general material) + 10 *Zusatzstoff* (class-specific material) | *Anlage 7 FeV* states 30 questions for class B *Ersterteilung*; the 20 + 10 split comes from the *Prüfungsrichtlinie* (examination directive), not from *Anlage 7* itself |
| Total points | 110 | *Anlage 7 FeV* |
| Weighting per question | 2 to 5 error points by safety relevance; the 5-pointers are mostly right-of-way and priority | *Anlage 7 FeV*; weightings 2–5 confirmed by DEGENER (click-learn.de) |
| Pass threshold | Maximum 10 error points | *Anlage 7 FeV*; [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/verkehr/rund-um-den-fuehrerschein/erwerb/theoretische-fuehrerscheinpruefung/) |
| Special fail rule | Two wrong 5-point questions = fail, despite being exactly 10 points | *Anlage 7 Nr. 1.2 FeV* |
| Time limit | **None** | ADAC: *"Es gibt keine zeitliche Begrenzung"*; [TÜV NORD](https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/services/driving-licence-and-vehicle/theory-test/): *"You decide yourself when to finalise the questionnaire."* |
| Typical duration | 20–45 minutes; allow up to about an hour at the *Prüfstelle* | ADAC (indicative, not a rule) |
| Medium | Computer — PC or tablet, in person at a test centre | TÜV NORD; nationwide PC rollout completed 1 January 2010 across roughly 665 test centres |
| Result | Immediate | DEKRA; TÜV NORD |

The two-wrong-fives rule is the single best-sourced fact in this document and the one that changes how you should study. It is codified in *Anlage 7 FeV*, whose operative clause the verification pass records as:

> … es sei denn, zwei Fragen mit Wertigkeit 5 falsch beantwortet

ADAC states the same thing from the other direction: *"Man darf jedoch nur eine mit fünf Fehlerpunkten bewertete Frage falsch beantworten"* — you may get at most **one** five-point question wrong. Note that the two research files render this clause slightly differently (the original researcher wrote *"Es sei denn, zwei Fragen mit Wertigkeit 5 wurden falsch beantwortet"*); the substance is identical, but if you need the exact statutory wording, read [*Anlage 7 FeV*](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/anlage_7.html) yourself rather than trusting either rendering.

Practically: the right-of-way questions are where the exam is won or lost. Two careless priority errors end the attempt even if everything else is perfect.

**Do not believe the 45-minute time limit.** Numerous app and SEO sites state one. The verification pass singles this out as the most commonly repeated error in the secondary literature on this topic; ADAC and TÜV NORD both confirm there is no limit.

### Question types

- **Video tasks** (since 2014): an exam contains **up to three** video tasks of roughly 15 seconds each, and they are counted **within** the 30 questions, not in addition to them (TÜV NORD). Each clip may be **replayed up to five times** before you answer — TÜV NORD: *"you have the option of watching the video sequences up to five times before answering the corresponding question."* Once the answer options open, replays stop. Some sites claim a single viewing; that is wrong.
- **Image-selection tasks** (*Abbildungsauswahlaufgaben*), introduced 1 April 2025: multiple correct answers can be selected, mostly on traffic signs (TÜV-Verband: *"Abbildungsauswahlaufgaben, bei denen mehrere Antworten ausgewählt werden können"*).
- ⚠️ **Not confirmed:** the original research also described a "film question with numeric input" as a 1 April 2025 novelty. The verification pass found no support for this in the TÜV-Verband release or the DEGENER commentary and instructed that it be removed. Treat it as non-existent.
- ⚠️ **Unverified mechanic:** whether you can mark a question and revisit it before submitting. This is widely repeated but the verification pass found no official documentation from *arge tp 21*, DEKRA or TÜV NORD. Assume nothing and ask at check-in.

## The question catalogue and its update cycle

The official catalogue (*amtlicher Fragenkatalog*) is produced by **TÜV | DEKRA arge tp 21**, the working group of the *Technische Prüfstellen* (technical examination centres), at [fahrerlaubnis.tuev-dekra.de](https://fahrerlaubnis.tuev-dekra.de/en/). ⚠️ **Corrected attribution:** the original research named the federal transport ministry (BMV/BMDV) as a co-developer. The TÜV-Verband release names the partners as *"die zuständigen Behörden, die Technischen Prüfstellen für den Kraftfahrzeugverkehr und die Fahrlehrerschaft"* — the competent authorities, the examination centres and the driving-instructor profession. The ministry's role is regulatory approval, not co-authorship.

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Class B pool, before 1 April 2025 | 1,197 questions |
| Class B pool, since 1 April 2025 | **1,040 questions** |
| Change volume | 123 questions removed; 64 new added, of which 48 replaced older ones; 15 of the new ones class-B-specific |
| New topics in that update | E-scooters, cargo bikes, pedelecs, the new THC limit |
| Normal update rhythm | Twice a year, 1 April and 1 October |
| 1 October 2025 | Deliberately **skipped** |
| 1 April 2026 | **No change** |
| Next possible change | 1 October 2026 at the earliest |

Source for the counts: [TÜV-Verband press release](https://www.tuev-verband.de/pressemitteilungen/neue-theoriefragen-fuer-den-fuehrerschein-aenderungen-ab-april) — *"Der Fragenkatalog für die Klasse B umfasste bislang 1.197 Fragen. Ab dem 1. April werden es 1.040 Fragen sein."*

**Conflict, resolved.** Driving-school sources (fahrschule.de, fahrschule-online.de) publish 1,169 for class B after the same update. The verification pass instructs that 1,040 be published as a firm figure and 1,169 dropped: TÜV-Verband speaks for the body that maintains the catalogue and quotes both the before and after numbers, while the driving-school figure comes with a change breakdown (17 new / 47 changed / 72 removed) that does not reconcile with the 123-removed / 64-new figures reported by TÜV and DEGENER. Use **1,040**.

The October 2025 skip was a joint decision by *arge tp 21*, the federal transport ministry and the state authorities, to give schools, examiners and authorities implementation time for **OFSA 2** (the next generation of the examination system); both the theory catalogue and the practical driving-task catalogue were left untouched. For April 2026, theorie24's own help centre states that *"Neue Fragen kann es daher erst ab den 1. Oktober 2026 geben."*

**What this means for you, and it is genuinely useful:** the catalogue in force for an exam taken in August 2026 is the 1 April 2025 version, and it cannot legally change before 1 October 2026. Any app or book advertising the April 2025 catalogue is current. This is the least stale part of the whole picture — for now.

**Scheduled to change (not in force).** The driving-training reform approved by the federal cabinet on 10 June 2026 plans to shrink the question catalogue and to replace the 2–5-point weighting with **one point per question** (OFSA 2). Both the amending law and the ordinance are still in the parliamentary process, so nothing has changed for a 2026 candidate. This is the item most likely to date this document.

## Exam languages

*Anlage 7 Nr. 1.3 FeV* permits the theory exam in German plus 12 foreign languages. The list is closed and reads exactly:

> Englisch, Französisch, Griechisch, Italienisch, Polnisch, Portugiesisch, Rumänisch, Russisch, Kroatisch, Spanisch, Türkisch, Hocharabisch

| Language | Available | Relevance to you |
|---|---|---|
| German | Yes | The default; also the only language with audio support |
| **English** | **Yes** | Confirmed |
| **Spanish** | **Yes** | Confirmed |
| **Italian** | **Yes** | Confirmed |
| French, Greek, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Croatian, Turkish, Modern Standard Arabic (*Hocharabisch*) | Yes | — |

*arge tp 21* independently states that exams run "in German and 12 further languages", and its public mock-exam picker offers both English and Italian. TÜV Rheinland's Berlin-Schöneberg centre advertises PC exams in German, English, Italian and the other official languages.

Three caveats:

1. **Audio is German-only.** Headphone audio support exists for the German version only. ⚠️ The verification pass could not confirm this first-hand and asks that you check it with your *Prüfstelle* before relying on it. If you were counting on hearing the questions read out in Spanish, do not.
2. **The practical exam is German-only.** *Anlage 7 Nr. 1.3 FeV* has no counterpart for the practical exam. Interpreter arrangements are a separate matter and are outside the scope of this document.
3. **Choose one language and stay in it.** All licensed learning products use the official TÜV | DEKRA translations, so the exam wording is identical to your app's wording — provided you study in the language you will be tested in. Expat sources warn that the official English is stilted; All About Berlin calls it "really bad". Given that you read Spanish and Italian natively and English fluently, it is worth doing a few mock exams in two of them and picking whichever official translation grates least, rather than assuming English is the obvious choice.

**Scheduled to change (not in force).** An amending ordinance adds **two** further languages to *Anlage 7 FeV*: **Ukrainian and Kurmanji** (*Kurmandschi*, Kurdish). ⚠️ The original research recorded only Ukrainian; the verification pass corrected this. Chronology: federal cabinet decision 27 May 2026; Bundesrat approval as item 61 of the 1067th session on 10 July 2026 (BR-Drs. 318/26); entry into force planned for later in 2026. As of 4 August 2026 the consolidated *Anlage 7 FeV* still lists only the 12 languages. Irrelevant to you in substance, but a useful signal that the annex does move.

### How you get in the door

1. **You name your *Prüfstelle* in the LABO application.** On the conversion route you must state the name and address of your *Fahrschule* and of your chosen *Prüfstelle* when you file at LABO — and, per the application requirements recorded in `raw/ar-legal-core.json`: *"Nach Erhalt der Prüfungszulassung ist ein Wechsel der Prüfstelle nicht mehr möglich"* (once the examination authorisation has been issued, the *Prüfstelle* can no longer be changed). Choose deliberately, before you file.
2. **The *Fahrschule* submits the exam registration** (*Prüfauftrag*) once LABO has issued your *Prüfungsaufforderung*.
3. **No appointment is needed for the theory exam.** DEKRA Berlin-Hohenschönhausen, verbatim: *"Für die Theorieprüfung brauchen Sie KEINEN Termin, kommen Sie einfach vorbei!"* You walk in during theory-exam hours.
4. **Transfer the fee at least 10 working days ahead.** Same source: *"überweisen Sie vorab die Gebühren (mindestens 10 Arbeitstage vor der Prüfung)."* Because the exam needs no appointment, candidates assume no lead time is required — and then cannot sit. ⚠️ This wording is DEKRA-Berlin-specific; TÜV Rheinland Berlin's payment practice was not separately verified, so ask when you register.

The **practical** exam, by contrast, is always scheduled by the *Fahrschule*.

**The school's own charge is unregulated.** On top of the official fee, a *Fahrschule* may levy a *Vorstellungsentgelt* (presentation fee) for registering and presenting you. Because this item is school-specific and unregulated, treat the range as an expectation and read the individual *Preisliste* — and note that on the conversion route you are buying registration and optional lessons, not a training package, so a school that bundles theory instruction into a *Grundbetrag* is charging you for something *FeV § 31 Abs. 2* says you do not need.

ADAC's EUR 3,500–6,000 anchor for a whole class B licence, with Berlin at the upper end, is quoted here only so you recognise it when you meet it. It describes a first licence, not your route.

## Failing, retaking, and how long a pass lasts

| Rule | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum wait before a retake | **14 days** (two weeks) | Statutory nationwide rule; ADAC states "zwei Wochen" |
| Maximum number of attempts | **None** | No statutory cap. The old three-attempt rule was abolished years ago |
| Cost per retake | The full EUR 24.99 again, plus any school presentation fee | *GebOSt* Nr. 401.1 + 401.3 |
| After a cheating attempt (*Täuschungshandlung*) | The exam counts as failed and the waiting period can extend **up to nine months** | *FeV § 18* |
| Validity of a pass | The practical exam must be taken within **12 months** of passing the theory | see conflict below |
| After passing the practical | The licence must be issued within **two years**, or the whole examination lapses | ⚠️ *FeV § 18* per the verification pass — section number not independently confirmed |
| Order | The practical exam may only be taken **after** the theory exam is passed | *FeV § 17 Abs. 1 S. 5* |

⚠️ **Unresolved conflict on the legal home of the 12-month window.** The verification pass attributes it to *FeV § 18* and quotes *"Die praktische Prüfung muss innerhalb von zwölf Monaten nach Bestehen der theoretischen Prüfung abgelegt werden."* The conversion-route research (`raw/ar-legal-core.json`) instead attributes it to *FeV § 22 Abs. 5* and frames it as the lapsing of the *Prüfauftrag*: the *Prüfauftrag* expires if the theory exam is not passed within 12 months of it reaching the *Technische Prüfstelle*, **or** the practical within 12 months of passing the theory. The **12-month duration is not in doubt** — both agree, and it is the number that matters. The section number is. The *§ 22 Abs. 5* framing is the more useful one for you because it adds a second clock you would otherwise miss (the *Prüfauftrag* itself ages out), but I could not resolve which provision the sentence actually sits in. Read [*FeV § 18*](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__18.html) and [*FeV § 22*](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__22.html) if you need to cite it.

⚠️ **Do not believe** that a *verkehrspsychologische Beratung* (traffic-psychology counselling session) becomes mandatory after three failed theory attempts. This circulates on German SEO sites and has no basis in the FeV, which sets no cap on attempts at all.

Two practical readings. First, a fortnight between attempts is short enough that failing is expensive in money and momentum but not in schedule. Second, because a theory pass starts a 12-month clock on the practical exam, and because you have no mandatory driving lessons forcing you to build up hours first, there is a real argument for sitting the theory exam only once you have a realistic plan for the practical — not the moment you feel ready for the questions.

## Mandatory theory lessons — and why they do not apply to you

For a **first** licence (*Ersterteilung*), *FahrschAusbO 2012 § 4* requires, before admission to the theory exam:

| Part | Amount |
|---|---|
| *Grundstoff* (general material, Anlage 1) | At least **12** *Doppelstunden* |
| *Zusatzstoff* class B (class-specific material, Anlage 2) | **2** *Doppelstunden* |
| Total | 14 units, i.e. the equivalent of about **21 teaching hours** of content |

⚠️ **Two corrections to the original research.** The instrument is the *Fahrschüler-Ausbildungsordnung* of **2012**, not a 2018 version. And § 4 permits delivery in single 45-minute lessons as well as 90-minute *Doppelstunden*, so "14 × 90 minutes" should be understood as a content volume rather than a fixed block structure. Holders of an existing licence adding a class take a reduced *Grundstoff*.

**None of this binds you.** *FeV § 31 Abs. 2*, verbatim:

> Beantragt der Inhaber einer Fahrerlaubnis aus einem nicht in Anlage 11 aufgeführten Staat unter den Voraussetzungen des Absatzes 1 Satz 1 und 2 die Erteilung einer Fahrerlaubnis für die entsprechende Klasse von Kraftfahrzeugen, sind die Vorschriften über die Ausbildung nicht anzuwenden.

Because Argentina is absent from *Anlage 11 FeV*, this is your provision. It makes the *FahrschAusbO* inapplicable: no mandatory theory block, no mandatory *Sonderfahrten*, no minimum number of driving lessons. What it does **not** waive is the examination itself (*FeV § 15*), the eyesight test (*FeV § 12 Abs. 2*) or first-aid training (*FeV § 19*) — those are switched off only by *FeV § 31 Abs. 1*, which is reserved for *Anlage 11* states.

The catch is that the exam still tests the syllabus those 21 hours exist to deliver, in German road-law categories that do not map cleanly onto Argentine practice. You are exempt from the teaching, not from the content. Many conversion candidates buy a handful of theory lessons voluntarily for exactly this reason, and it is a reasonable use of money in a route where you are saving thousands.

**Scheduled to change (not in force, and irrelevant to you).** The federal cabinet approved a reform on 10 June 2026 (Transport Minister Patrick Schnieder) that drops the *Präsenzpflicht* — the in-person attendance requirement — for theory lessons, allowing fully online or hybrid theory delivered by licensed *Fahrschulen* with identity and attendance checks; self-study apps will **not** replace instruction. Both measures are *"nun im parlamentarischen Verfahren"*, so as of August 2026 in-person attendance still applies to everyone it applies to — which does not include you.

## What to bring, and what happens on the day

DEKRA requires, verbatim, *"Personalausweis oder Reisepass oder das von der Fahrerlaubnisbehörde benannte Personaldokument"* — an ID card or passport, or the identity document named by the licensing authority — plus the driving school's training certification.

| Bring | Note |
|---|---|
| Your **Italian passport** (or Italian *carta d'identità*) | An Italian ID card is a valid EU identity document, but the document *named by LABO* governs. The passport is the safe choice. |
| Your **LABO paperwork** — *Prüfungsaufforderung* / *Prüfungszulassung* | This is what establishes your admission on the conversion route, in place of a training certificate. |
| **Proof the fee was paid** | In Berlin normally the bank transfer confirmation. |
| Glasses or contact lenses if you need them | Not checked in the theory exam, but you will be reading a screen. |
| ⚠️ *Ausbildungsbescheinigung* (training certificate) | **Probably not required for you** — see below. |

⚠️ **The *Ausbildungsbescheinigung* question — the one thing to phone about.** Read literally, *FeV § 16 Abs. 3 S. 6–8* requires the candidate to hand the examiner a *Fahrschule* confirmation that the prescribed *FahrschAusbO* training was completed, and § 16 Abs. 3 S. 8 says that without it the exam *"darf nicht durchgeführt werden"* — may not be conducted. The systematic answer is that *FeV § 31 Abs. 2* renders the training rules inapplicable, so there is no training capable of being certified and the confirmation cannot be demanded; secondary sources agree that no *Ausbildungsbescheinigung* is required for third-country conversion candidates. But the conversion research rates this at **medium** confidence and calls it the weakest link in its analysis. Because the examiner will refuse the exam if they insist on a document you cannot produce, confirm with **both** LABO and your *Fahrschule* when you register — not on the morning of the exam.

**Procedure.** Arrive during theory-exam hours, check in at reception, hand over ID and paperwork, take a seat at a PC or touchscreen in the exam room alongside other candidates, listen to a short system introduction, select your exam language, and answer the 30 questions. B197 candidates (automatic training plus manual competence) additionally need proof of *Schaltkompetenz* — relevant in Berlin, where automatic-only training is now common, and it affects what the final licence permits.

**Result.** Immediate: the system evaluates on submission and the result appears on screen. ⚠️ **Corrected:** delivery of the written record is **not** standardised. DEKRA hands over a protocol at the centre; TÜV NORD sends the result report by email and posts it in its learner-driver app. Expect "immediately available, on paper or electronically depending on the *Prüfstelle*" rather than a guaranteed printout. If you pass, the *Prüfstelle* records it and the 12-month clock for the practical exam starts.

| Metric (2025) | Value |
|---|---|
| Theory exams sat, all classes | over 2.04 million — a record |
| Theory failure rate, all classes | 41 % (unchanged vs 2024) |
| **Theory failure rate, class B / BF17** | **44 %** (down from 45 % in 2024) |
| Share of theory exams that were retakes | 38 % |
| First-attempt theory pass rate | 63 % |
| Practical exams sat | 1.89 million (+4.9 %) |
| Practical failure rate, all classes | 31 % |
| Practical failure rate, class B / BF17 | 37 % |
| First-attempt practical pass rate | 65 % |

For 2024: roughly 1.6 million class B theory exams, a 45 % class B theory failure rate, and 39 % of class B theory exams were retakes.

⚠️ **Dropped as unreproducible:** the original research also carried "91 % had passed by the third attempt" and a repeat-attempt pass rate of 44 % (i.e. 56 % of retakes failing). The verification pass could not reproduce either and instructed that they be dropped or re-sourced. Do not quote them.

Failure rates sitting near record highs is one of the stated arguments behind the 2026 reform to shrink the catalogue. The honest reading for you: this is a test that four in ten class B candidates fail, taken by people who have just sat through 21 hours of instruction on exactly this material. ## Study materials in English, Spanish and Italian
