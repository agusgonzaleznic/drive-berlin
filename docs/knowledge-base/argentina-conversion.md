# Converting an Argentine driving licence in Berlin

*Scope: a holder of a valid Argentine **licencia nacional de conducir** (car / class B equivalent) with **no** EU driving licence, whose Hauptwohnsitz is registered in Berlin. Where an EU passport changes the answer it is called out explicitly (Italian is used as the worked example). Little or no German is assumed.*

**Legal text state:** Fahrerlaubnis-Verordnung (FeV) vom 13. Dezember 2010 (BGBl. I S. 1980), last amended by Art. 7 G v. 12.5.2026 (BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 142). All statutes read directly from gesetze-im-internet.de on **2026-08-04**.
**Volatile figures** (LABO processing backlog, the Anlage 11 country list, pending ordinances) are dated inline and must be re-checked at the linked source. Two ordinances affecting this topic had been approved but **not yet promulgated** as of 2026-08-04.

> ### Bottom line
>
> 1. **Argentina is not in Anlage 11 FeV, so you must pass BOTH the German theory exam and the German practical exam** (§ 31 Abs. 2 FeV in conjunction with § 15 Abs. 1 FeV). Driving-school *training* is waived — no theory lessons, no Pflichtstunden, no 12 Sonderfahrten — but a Fahrschule is still unavoidable for the practical exam. Anyone who tells you an Argentine licence converts exam-free is wrong.
> 2. **Stop driving six months after you established your Hauptwohnsitz** (§ 29 Abs. 1 Satz 4 FeV). That clock is already running and cannot be extended in your situation. Driving past it is a **criminal offence** — Fahren ohne Fahrerlaubnis, up to one year's imprisonment or a fine (§ 21 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 StVG). Filing the application does *not* buy you driving time.
> 3. **Do the three cheap prerequisites this week, then file at any Berlin Bürgeramt:** a 9 × 45 min first-aid course (§ 19 Abs. 1 FeV), an eye test less than two years old (§ 12 Abs. 2 and Abs. 7 FeV), a certified translation of the Spanish licence, a **paper** biometric photo, and the names and addresses of your chosen **Fahrschule and Prüfstelle** (these must be on the form). Fee **45,10 €**. Berlin's third-country queue was ~8 weeks on 2026-08-04, so file now — but with a complete file, because processing starts only when everything is in and paid.
> 4. **Two hard document deadlines can destroy the whole route.** Your Argentine licence must still be **valid and physically present in the original** when you apply (§ 31 Abs. 3 FeV) — there is no rescue provision for expired third-country licences, unlike § 30 Abs. 2 for EU ones. And it must have been issued **before** you established residence in Germany (§ 29 Abs. 3 Nr. 2 FeV). Because Argentine licences get a fresh issue date at each ~5-year renewal, get documentary proof of the **original** issue date now.
> 5. **Do not convert in Italy.** Your Italian passport removes the residence-permit requirement from the Berlin file and lets you sit the theory exam in Italian or Spanish — but it does nothing for the licence. An Italian *patente* obtained by exam-free conversion of your Argentine licence would **not** be recognised in Germany (§ 28 Abs. 4 Satz 1 Nr. 7 and Nr. 8 FeV), and Italy could not lawfully issue one while your residence is Berlin. There is no shortcut. Budget ~500–1,400 € and 3–6 months for the Berlin route — against 2.466–4.476 € for a full first German licence.

## The legal verdict

### Argentina is not in Anlage 11 FeV

Verified by downloading and reading the complete official consolidated text of Anlage 11 FeV on 2026-08-04 (<https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/anlage_11.html> — note the path is `/fev_2010/`, the shorter `/fev/` returns HTTP 404). A full-text search for "Argentin" returns **zero matches**. In fact **no South American state appears in Anlage 11 at all**. The list contains, verbatim: Albanien, Andorra, Bosnien und Herzegowina, Französisch-Polynesien, Gibraltar, Guernsey, Insel Man, Israel, Japan, Jersey, Kosovo, Moldau, Republik Nordmazedonien, Monaco, Namibia, Neukaledonien, Neuseeland, Republik Korea, San Marino, Schweiz, Serbien, Singapur, Südafrika, Taiwan, Vereinigtes Königreich, plus named Australian territories, Canadian provinces, US states and Japanese-style entries.

Argentina is therefore a **Drittstaat** (third country) and the general rule of § 31 Abs. 2 FeV applies.

### What § 31 Abs. 2 FeV actually waives — and what it does not

> **§ 31 Abs. 2 FeV, verbatim:** „Beantragt der Inhaber einer Fahrerlaubnis aus einem nicht in Anlage 11 aufgeführten Staat unter den Voraussetzungen des Absatzes 1 Satz 1 und 2 die Erteilung einer Fahrerlaubnis für die entsprechende Klasse von Kraftfahrzeugen, sind die Vorschriften über die Ausbildung nicht anzuwenden."

That waives **exactly one thing**: the training rules. Compare § 31 Abs. 1 Satz 1, which applies only to Anlage-11 states and switches off **five** things:

| § 31 Abs. 1 Satz 1 Nr. | What it disapplies | Waived for Argentina? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | § 11 Abs. 9 and § 12 Abs. 6 (medical / eye-specialist examinations) | Not relevant to class B anyway |
| 2 | § 12 Abs. 2 — **the Sehtest** | **NO** |
| 3 | § 15 — **the Befähigungsprüfung** (theory + practical exam) | **NO** |
| 4 | § 19 — **the first-aid course** | **NO** |
| 5 | die Vorschriften über die Ausbildung (driving-school curriculum) | **YES** |

Because § 31 Abs. 2 imports only item 5, the eye test and the first-aid course are **federal statutory requirements**, not a Berlin add-on. They would apply in every Bundesland; applying in another state would not avoid them.

Note also the condition hidden in the ellipsis that most summaries drop: § 31 Abs. 2 applies only *"unter den Voraussetzungen des Absatzes 1 Satz 1 und 2"*, and § 31 Abs. 1 Satz 1 requires a licence that *„zum Führen von Kraftfahrzeugen im Inland berechtigt **oder dazu berechtigt hat**"*. The past tense matters twice: it means a lapsed six-month window does **not** destroy the training waiver, but a licence that never entitled you to drive here (see § 29 Abs. 3 Nr. 2, below) cannot be converted at all.

### Both exams are mandatory

> **§ 15 Abs. 1 FeV, verbatim:** „Der Bewerber um eine Fahrerlaubnis hat seine Befähigung in einer theoretischen und einer praktischen Prüfung nachzuweisen."

§ 15 is switched off only by § 31 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 (Anlage-11 states, and then only *"nach Maßgabe der Anlage 11"*). § 31 Abs. 2 does not touch it. § 15 Abs. 2–4 contain the only exemptions (class L, BE/C1E/DE/D1E extensions, A1→A2→A upgrades) — none covers class B from a foreign licence.

This was verified five times: the primary statute plus four independent sources, three of which are official licensing authorities:

- **ADAC:** „Führerscheininhaber aus Drittstaaten, die nicht in der Staatenliste aufgeführt sind, müssen eine theoretische und praktische Führerscheinprüfung machen." ([source](https://www.adac.de/verkehr/rund-um-den-fuehrerschein/auslaendische-fuehrerscheine/staaten-auserhalb/))
- **LABO Berlin** (your competent authority): „Wenn Ihr Führerschein nicht in einem EU-/EWR-Land und nicht in einem Land aus der Anlage 11 ausgestellt wurde, müssen Sie bei der Umschreibung theoretische und praktische Prüfungen für alle Klassen, die Sie umschreiben wollen, ablegen." ([source](https://www.berlin.de/labo/mobilitaet/fahrerlaubnisse-personen-und-gueterbefoerderung/aktuelles/artikel.1445533.php))
- **Stadt Köln:** „Sie müssen eine theoretische und praktische Führerscheinprüfung ablegen"
- **Landratsamt Fürstenfeldbruck:** „müssen Sie die theoretische und praktische Prüfung für die jeweilige(n) Fahrerlaubnisklasse(n) ablegen."

### Training is waived — a Fahrschule is not

No mandatory theory-lesson block (normally 14 Doppelstunden), no Sonderfahrten (normally 12: 5 Überland, 4 Autobahn, 3 Nachtfahrt), **no minimum number of driving lessons at all**. ADAC: „Sie müssen jedoch keine Fahrschulausbildung durchlaufen. Es sind keine Mindest- oder Pflicht-Fahrstunden erforderlich."

The pending FahrschModV draft codifies this expressly — draft new FahrschAusbV § 13 Abs. 1: *„Diese Verordnung ist nicht anzuwenden, wenn … 4. die Fahrerlaubnis auf Grund einer ausländischen Fahrerlaubnis nach § 31 Absatz 1 oder 2 der Fahrerlaubnis-Verordnung erteilt werden soll."* (BR-Drs. 365/26, Artikel 1 — **not yet in force**.)

But you cannot take the practical exam without a driving school, for three independent legal reasons:

1. **§ 17 Abs. 1 Satz 3–4 FeV** puts the duty on *you*: „Der Bewerber hat ein der Anlage 7 entsprechendes Prüfungsfahrzeug für die Klasse bereitzustellen … Darüber hinaus hat er die für die Durchführung der Prüfung notwendigen Materialien bereitzustellen."
2. **Anlage 7 Nr. 2.2.16 FeV** requires dual controls (Doppelbedienungseinrichtungen), an extra interior mirror and two right-hand exterior mirrors, and seats for examiner, Fahrlehrer and candidate — a private car cannot comply.
3. **StVG § 2 Abs. 15** requires a Fahrlehrer in the car for every examination drive, and treats the Fahrlehrer as the legal driver when the person at the wheel has no corresponding Fahrerlaubnis. This is also why lessons and exam drives remain lawful *after* your six months expire.

Berlin says it plainly: *„Für die praktische Prüfung brauchen Sie eine Fahrschule."* And the Berlin application form requires the name and address of both the **Fahrschule** and the **Prüfstelle**, because the Prüfauftrag is posted directly to the Prüfstelle.

⚠️ **Unverified, worth one question:** § 16 Abs. 3 Satz 6–8 and § 17 Abs. 5 Satz 5 FeV normally require the candidate to hand the examiner a Fahrschule confirmation that the prescribed FahrschAusbO training was completed, and § 16 Abs. 3 Satz 8 says the exam *„darf nicht durchgeführt werden"* without it. The correct systematic reading is that this cannot be demanded of you, because § 31 Abs. 2 makes the Ausbildungsvorschriften inapplicable — there is no training capable of being certified. This is the weakest link in the whole analysis (confidence: medium). Confirm with LABO **and** with your Fahrschule before booking the theory exam, because the exam is refused if the examiner insists on a document you cannot produce.

### The eye test and the first-aid course

**Sehtest — required.** § 12 Abs. 2 Satz 1 FeV: „Bewerber um eine Fahrerlaubnis der Klassen AM, A1, A2, A, B, BE, L oder T haben sich einem Sehtest zu unterziehen." Berlin's checklist: *„Drittstaaten: Bei der Umschreibung einer Pkw- oder Motorradklasse ist immer ein Sehtest erforderlich."* Carried out by an amtlich anerkannte Sehteststelle (most opticians) under DIN 58220 Teil 6; passed at 0,7 on each eye with or without correction (Anlage 6 Nr. 1.1). It may be repeated with better glasses; failing it triggers a mandatory ophthalmological examination (§ 12 Abs. 5), and an ophthalmologist's certificate can replace it entirely (§ 12 Abs. 4). **§ 12 Abs. 7: the certificate must not be older than two years at application** — so do not do it months in advance.

**Erste-Hilfe-Kurs — required.** § 19 Abs. 1 Satz 1 FeV: „Bewerber um eine Fahrerlaubnis müssen an einer Schulung in Erster Hilfe teilnehmen, die mindestens neun Unterrichtseinheiten zu je 45 Minuten umfasst." § 21 Abs. 3 Nr. 5 FeV requires the certificate to be attached to the application. Berlin: *„Nachweis über Schulung in Erster Hilfe (Drittstaat) — Nur bei der Umschreibung eines Führerscheins aus einem Drittstaat erforderlich."*

**This is the single most commonly mis-stated fact in the whole topic.** Driving schools and forums routinely say the first-aid course is "only for a first licence, not for a conversion". That is a correct generalisation **only** for EU/EEA conversions (§ 30 Abs. 1 Nr. 4) and Anlage-11 conversions (§ 31 Abs. 1 Nr. 4), where § 19 is expressly waived. It is **false for Argentina**.

Two useful details: the first-aid certificate has **no expiry date** (LABO: „Die Ausbildung in 1. Hilfe hat kein Ablaufdatum"), unlike the eye test — so an old certificate is still usable. And § 19 Abs. 3 exempts doctors, dentists (**including those with foreign medical or dental training** — directly relevant if applicable), graduates of certain regulated health professions, holders of Sanitäts-/rettungsdienstliche training, and holders of a Deutsches Rettungsschwimmabzeichen in Silber or Gold. Check before paying.

### The two document conditions that can end the route

**1. The Argentine licence must still be valid, in the original.** § 31 Abs. 3 FeV: „Der Antragsteller hat den Besitz der ausländischen Fahrerlaubnis durch den nationalen Führerschein nachzuweisen. Außerdem hat er seinem Antrag … eine Erklärung des Inhalts beizugeben, dass seine ausländische Fahrerlaubnis noch gültig ist. Die Fahrerlaubnisbehörde ist berechtigt, die Richtigkeit der Erklärung zu überprüfen." Berlin: *„Die ausländische Fahrerlaubnis muss zum Zeitpunkt der Antragstellung der deutschen Fahrerlaubnis noch gültig sein und im Original vorliegen."* and *„Internationale Führerscheine werden nicht umgeschrieben."*

**There is no rescue provision.** § 30 Abs. 2 FeV expressly saves expired EU/EEA licences; § 31 has no equivalent. If your Argentine licence expires before you file, the conversion route closes and you face a full German Ersterteilung with the complete mandatory curriculum (≈2.466–4.476 €). Argentine LNCs typically run five years and renewal generally requires appearing in person at a Centro Emisor in Argentina. **This is the largest single cost risk in your plan.**

**2. The licence must predate your German residence.** § 29 Abs. 3 Nr. 2 FeV excludes holders *„die zum Zeitpunkt der Erteilung der ausländischen Erlaubnis … ihren ordentlichen Wohnsitz im Inland hatten"*. Read verbatim, there is **no** student/pupil "six months abroad" exception for third-country licences — that escape exists only in Nr. 2a (EU/EEA licences) and in § 28 Abs. 4 Nr. 2 and Nr. 8. LABO's published gloss („vor Zuzug nach Deutschland oder während eines mindestens 6-monatigen Auslandsaufenthalts erworben") is an administrative accommodation, not a statutory exception — do not build a plan on it.

LABO warns explicitly: *„Im Zweifelsfall kann es erforderlich sein, Unterlagen über den Auslandsaufenthalt vorzulegen, oder nachzuweisen, dass die erste Fahrerlaubnis vor dem Zuzug nach Deutschland erworben wurde (wenn der aktuelle Führerschein später ausgestellt wurde)."* Since Argentine licences carry a **new issue date at each renewal**, the card in your pocket may well show a date after your Berlin Anmeldung. Get proof of the *original* issue date before you file:

- **Certificado de Legalidad Internacional de la licencia de conducir** — issued free by the Agencia Nacional de Seguridad Vial (ANSV), online via TAD (Trámites a Distancia) or in person at a Centro Emisor de Licencias, attaching both sides of the licence (max 20 MB). Official purpose: *„documento oficial que verifica que tu licencia es auténtica y se encuentra vigente"*, expressly for *„quienes se radiquen en otro país y tramiten su licencia de conducir"*. [ANSV page](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/servicio/obtener-el-certificado-de-legalidad-internacional-de-la-licencia-de-conducir)
  - **Two hard scope limits, verbatim from that page:** *„no se incluyen las licencias otorgadas bajo legislación municipal y/o provincial"* and *„no se certificarán licencias emitidas antes de 2010."* For a provincial/municipal licence the page directs you to *„el Centro Emisor de Licencia Nacional (CEL) que la emitió"*.
  - ⚠️ **Correction to a claim circulating in secondary sources:** the ANSV page does **not** mention a "Historial" document and does not contain the often-quoted phrase about *„la fecha de emisión de su Licencia de Conducir ORIGINAL"* — that wording comes from an Argentine consular page and was misattributed. If you need proof of the original issue date, ask the issuing **CEL** directly for a record of your licence history. Processing time for the Certificado is not stated on the official page — ⚠️ unverified.
- **A Meldebescheinigung showing the date of your first German Anmeldung.** Berlin does not list it, but § 22 Abs. 1 FeV lets the authority check the Melderegister, and Stadt Köln requires a *„Bestätigung über die erste Anmeldung in der Bundesrepublik Deutschland"* outright. Cheap insurance.

### Authenticity checks

The authority **must** obtain domestic register extracts at your cost — § 22 Abs. 2 Satz 2 FeV: *„Sie hat dazu auf seine Kosten eine Auskunft aus dem Fahreignungsregister und dem Zentralen Fahrerlaubnisregister einzuholen."* You cannot decline these; they are what the GebOSt Nr. 145 and Nr. 126.2 line items inside your 45,10 € pay for.

A query to **Argentina** is discretionary, not mandatory. § 22 Abs. 2 Satz 3 FeV: *„Sie **kann** außerdem auf seine Kosten – in der Regel über das Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt – eine Auskunft aus den entsprechenden ausländischen Registern einholen…"*; and Satz 4 lets the authority shift the burden onto you to produce the foreign register extract if there are doubts. Anlage 11 makes a KBA query *mandatory* only for pre-cutoff Albanian and Kosovan licences — there is no such footnote for Argentina, because Argentina is not in Anlage 11 at all. Berlin's published document list contains no authenticity certificate. ⚠️ Whether LABO routinely queries Argentine licences, at what cost and with what delay, is **unverified** — ask.

## Deadlines and the clock

### Six months to drive — then it is a crime

> **§ 29 Abs. 1 Satz 4 FeV, verbatim:** „Begründet der Inhaber einer in einem anderen Staat erteilten Fahrerlaubnis einen ordentlichen Wohnsitz im Inland, besteht die Berechtigung noch sechs Monate."

**When the clock starts.** The statute keys the six months to the **factual establishment of the ordentlicher Wohnsitz**, not to the administrative Anmeldung. § 7 Abs. 1 Satz 2 FeV defines it as living in Germany *„gewöhnlich, das heißt während mindestens 185 Tagen im Jahr"* because of personal and occupational ties. LABO and ADAC in practice treat the Anmeldung date as that date (LABO: *„Sobald Sie Ihren ordentlichen Wohnsitz (Hauptwohnsitz) in Deutschland anmelden, dürfen Sie noch sechs Monate fahren"*), and police check the Meldedatum. **Practical rule: count from the earlier of actual move-in and Anmeldung. A late Anmeldung does not reset the clock**, and § 22 Abs. 1 FeV lets the authority check the register.

⚠️ **Unverified:** whether short trips abroad interrupt or restart the period. The research indicated they do not (only giving up the German ordinary residence does), but this could not be confirmed against an official source.

**The extension does not help you.** § 29 Abs. 1 Satz 5 FeV: „Die Fahrerlaubnisbehörde **kann** die Frist auf Antrag **bis zu** sechs Monate verlängern, wenn der Antragsteller glaubhaft macht, dass er seinen ordentlichen Wohnsitz **nicht länger als zwölf Monate** im Inland haben wird." It is discretionary, capped at six further months, and presupposes you will *leave* within twelve months. As someone settling in Berlin you cannot make that showing. LABO also states flatly: *„Die Fahrberechtigung von sechs Monaten kann nicht verlängert werden."* In Berlin the application would go to LABO, Puttkamerstr. 16–18; it is *gebührenpflichtig* and *„entsprechende Nachweise über Ihren Aufenthalt müssen vorgelegt werden"*. ⚠️ The exact fee is **unverified** — the GebOSt tariff contains no dedicated item; Nr. 201 (5,10 €, „Prüfung eines Antrags auf Erteilung des Rechts, von einer ausländischen Fahrerlaubnis im Inland Gebrauch zu machen") is the plausible basis.

**You are not a Berufspendler.** The BMDV Merkblatt describes the only route to unlimited recognition: *„Berufspendler begründen keinen ordentlichen Wohnsitz in der Bundesrepublik Deutschland. Ihr ausländischer Führerschein wird, solange er selbst gültig ist, ohne zeitliche Begrenzung in der Bundesrepublik Deutschland anerkannt."* It then expressly excludes people who took up employment in Germany and only occasionally return to a family residence abroad. With a Hauptwohnsitz in Berlin you are not a commuter and the six months run.

### There is no deadline for the conversion application itself

LABO: *„Es gibt keine Frist, in der dieser Antrag gestellt werden muss. Zu beachten ist nur, dass der ausländische Führerschein bei Antragstellung im Original vorliegen und noch gültig sein muss."* Confirmed by ADAC (*„Eine Frist für den Antrag gibt es nicht"*) and by the BMDV Merkblatt, which states that non-Anlage-11 licences can still be converted *„unter erleichterten Bedingungen"* after the six months. The statutory hook is the past tense in § 31 Abs. 1 Satz 1 (*„berechtigt oder dazu berechtigt hat"*).

**The widely repeated "you must convert within three years" rule does not exist.** Nothing in §§ 28–31 FeV or Anlage 11 imposes a three-year — or any — conversion deadline.

So: the six months bar **driving**, not **applying**. Missing them is bad but not fatal to the conversion.

### The hard deadlines that appear once the process starts

| Deadline | Legal basis | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Retake of a failed exam: not before an appropriate interval, **as a rule at least two weeks** (up to **nine months** where cheating is found) | § 18 Abs. 1 FeV | Wait |
| **Practical exam within 12 months of passing the theory** | § 18 Abs. 2 Satz 1–2 FeV: *„Andernfalls verliert die theoretische Prüfung ihre Gültigkeit."* | Theory pass void; retake in full |
| **Maximum 2 years between completing the practical exam and handover of the Führerschein** | § 18 Abs. 2 Satz 3–4 FeV: *„Andernfalls verliert die gesamte Prüfung ihre Gültigkeit."* | Entire exam void |
| Theory within 12 months of the Prüfauftrag reaching the Prüfstelle; practical within 12 months of the theory pass | § 22 Abs. 5 FeV (a *soll* directive to the Technische Prüfstelle) | Prüfauftrag returned to LABO |

§ 18 Abs. 2 is the **binding, self-executing** rule; § 22 Abs. 5 is the administrative consequence. Many guides cite only § 22 Abs. 5 and thereby understate the position. There is **no cap on the number of attempts** (§ 18 FeV sets none; ADAC confirms).

Berlin requires a **separate LABO appointment** to hand over the licence, so the two-year window in § 18 Abs. 2 Satz 3 is not merely theoretical — do not let it drift.

⚠️ **Change flag:** BR-Drs. 365/26 (FahrschModV) would **delete § 18 Abs. 2 entirely** — Begründung: *„Der bisherige Absatz 2 wird gestrichen. Der bisherige zeitliche Zusammenhang zwischen theoretischer und praktischer Fahrerlaubnisprüfung erweist sich als entbehrlich."* — and replace the 12+12-month Prüfauftrag structure with a **single five-year window** in § 22 Abs. 5. Target date **1 July 2027**, and the Drucksache is internally inconsistent about its own entry-into-force numbering. **Not promulgated as of 2026-08-04.** Plan on today's law.

### If the windows expire on this path

The Prüfauftrag returns to LABO, the prepared Führerschein goes back with it, and in practice a fresh application with fresh authority fees under GebOSt Nr. 201/202 is required — plus a fresh § 31 Abs. 3 declaration that the Argentine licence is *still* valid. ⚠️ The Berlin re-application mechanics are **unverified**; ask LABO in writing (a) how long your Prüfauftrag is valid, (b) exactly what must be resubmitted if it lapses, and (c) whether the Argentine licence must still be valid at the time of the new application.

### The criminal risk of driving past the six months

> **§ 21 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 StVG, verbatim:** „Mit Freiheitsstrafe bis zu einem Jahr oder mit Geldstrafe wird bestraft, wer 1. ein Kraftfahrzeug führt, obwohl er die dazu erforderliche Fahrerlaubnis nicht hat …"

- **Negligent commission is also punishable** — § 21 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 StVG: up to six months' imprisonment or a fine of up to 180 Tagessätze. Genuinely miscounting the six months is negligence, not a defence.
- **The vehicle keeper commits the same offence.** § 21 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 StVG criminalises the Halter who orders or allows the drive — relevant for a partner's car, an employer's car or car-sharing.
- **This is a Straftat, not a fine.** A conviction produces a **criminal record entry** — materially worse than "up to one year or a fine" suggests, and capable of interacting with employment matters even for an EU citizen.
- **Confiscation is narrower than usually reported.** § 21 Abs. 3 StVG permits Einziehung of the vehicle only where the offender drove despite a withdrawn licence / Fahrverbot / Sperre, permitted it as Halter in those circumstances, or was already convicted under Abs. 1 within the preceding three years. A first-time offender who merely let the six months lapse meets none of those.
- **Fahreignungsregister points:** three points if an Entziehung or isolierte Sperre was ordered, otherwise two (Anlage 13 FeV Nr. 1.11 / 2.1.11).
- **A court could block your German licence.** § 69a Abs. 1 Satz 3 StGB allows an *isolierte Sperre* of six months to five years for someone holding no licence — and § 69a Abs. 1 Satz 2 permits it **indefinitely** *„wenn zu erwarten ist, daß die gesetzliche Höchstfrist zur Abwehr der von dem Täter drohenden Gefahr nicht ausreicht."* However, this is **not automatic**: it presupposes the court would have withdrawn a licence under § 69 StGB for unfitness, and § 21 StVG is not among the Regelfälle of § 69 Abs. 2 StGB. Possible, far from certain, for a first lapse.
- **Insurance.** Your Kfz-Haftpflicht insurer must still compensate the injured third party (§ 117 VVG), but driving without the prescribed licence breaches a policy obligation and the insurer can recover **up to 5.000 €** from you (§ 5 KfzPflVV). ⚠️ Teil-/Vollkasko is reported to refuse cover in these cases (the 5.000 € cap applies only to compulsory liability, not voluntary cover) — **unverified**.
- **The authority can formally declare you have no entitlement.** § 29 Abs. 3 Satz 2 FeV: *„In den Fällen des Satzes 1 kann die Behörde einen feststellenden Verwaltungsakt über die fehlende Berechtigung erlassen."* GebOSt Nr. 206 prices that at 33,20–256,00 €.
- ⚠️ **Change flag (negative):** § 21 StVG has **not** been decriminalised. The 2025/26 Bundestag "Entkriminalisierung" debate concerned fare-dodging (§ 265a StGB), and those bills were rejected on 16 April 2026.

### Applying in time buys you no driving time

The entitlement revives only when the German Führerschein is **physically handed over**, or exceptionally on a temporary Prüfungsbescheinigung under Anlage 8a FeV (§ 22 Abs. 4 Satz 6 FeV). Nothing about a pending application, an issued Prüfauftrag or a passed exam restores the right to drive.

**Berlin trap — the most dangerous operational detail in this document.** In a Drittstaat conversion the examiner does **not** hand you a driving entitlement. You receive a **Prüfbescheinigung**, and then need a **separate LABO appointment** — LABO FAQ: *„Bitte vereinbaren Sie … einen Termin und bringen zu diesem die Originale Prüfbescheinigung mit. Sie erhalten **in der Führerscheinstelle** eine vorläufige Fahrberechtigung"*, and *„Bei der Umschreibung einer Fahrerlaubnis aus einem Drittstaat sind sowohl die Originale Prüfbescheinigung als auch das Original des ausländischen Führerscheins in der Fahrerlaubnisbehörde vorzulegen."* **Treat the gap between passing the practical exam and holding the vorläufige Fahrberechtigung as no-drive**, and get LABO to confirm otherwise in writing if you want to rely on anything else.

### What you must carry while the Argentine licence is still valid

§ 29 Abs. 2 FeV requires a foreign national licence not drawn up in German, and not conforming to Anhang 6 of the 1968 Vienna Convention, to be **carried together with a translation** — which *„muss von einem international anerkannten Automobilklub des Ausstellungsstaates oder einer vom Bundesministerium für Verkehr und digitale Infrastruktur bestimmten Stelle gefertigt sein."*

- Argentina is a party to the **1949 Geneva** Convention but **not** to the 1968 Vienna Convention, so the Annex-6 escape is unavailable and an Argentine IDP is a 1949-type permit, which § 29 Abs. 2 Satz 1 FeV expressly accepts.
- Germany has waived the translation requirement only for **Andorra, Hong Kong, Monaco, New Zealand, San Marino, Switzerland and Senegal**. Argentina is not on that list.
- An **International Driving Permit replaces the translation**, but only together with the national licence — an IDP alone is never enough and can never be converted. It must be obtained **in Argentina**; Germany will not issue one on a foreign licence. The ANSV page itself points to the club: *„para conducir en otro país, se recomienda llevar siempre la licencia internacional que otorga el Automóvil Club Argentino."*
- **Missing translation is only a minor Ordnungswidrigkeit** — 10 € Verwarnungsgeld standard rate under § 75 Nr. 4 FeV. Do **not** confuse this with Fahren ohne Fahrerlaubnis. Accepted translators: German automobile clubs (ADAC), court-appointed and generally sworn translators, internationally recognised automobile clubs of the issuing state, and official bodies of the issuing state.

## Step-by-step Berlin procedure

**Official page:** <https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/327537/> — *„Fahrerlaubnis – Umschreibung einer ausländischen Fahrerlaubnis aus einem Nicht-EU/EWR-Land (Drittstaat/Anlage 11)"*. There is **no English version**: `/327537/en/` returns HTTP 404 (verified 2026-08-04). Do **not** use `/dienstleistung/121598/` — that is the EU/EEA service and does not apply to you.

**Prerequisites:** Hauptwohnsitz in Berlin (done); *„Persönliche Vorsprache ist erforderlich."* There is **no online or postal route**. FAO (Führerschein Antrag Online) covers only Ersterteilung, Erweiterung and B197 — not Umschreibung; ⚠️ this is an argument from absence on LABO's own FAO page (confidence: medium), so it is worth one question to your Fahrschule.

### 0. Before anything: verify the two killer conditions
Confirm (a) the expiry date of your Argentine licence and that it will still be valid at application, and (b) that your **first** Argentine licence was issued while you were resident in Argentina, with documentary proof (§ 31 Abs. 3, § 29 Abs. 3 Nr. 2 FeV). Nothing else is worth spending money on until these are settled.

### 1. Order the translation
Certified translation of the Spanish licence, ideally with **Klassifizierung** (mapping the Argentine category onto German classes). ADAC is the route the authority and schools recommend: online via the ADAC-Shop with photos of both sides, collection at a chosen branch after 5–7 working days, open to non-members; or in person. Alternatives: a court-sworn translator ([justiz-uebersetzer.de](https://www.justiz-uebersetzer.de/)) or an official body of Argentina.

Berlin's rule is conditional: a translation is required only where the licence is *„nicht in deutscher **oder englischer** Sprache"* — Spanish qualifies, so yes — and *„Ob eine Übersetzung … erforderlich ist, entscheidet im Zweifel die Fahrerlaubnisbehörde. Die Übersetzung wird dann bei der Bearbeitung des Antrages nachgefordert."* Technically you may file without it, but **processing does not begin until the file is complete and paid** (LABO: *„Voraussetzung für die Bearbeitung von Anträgen ist, dass der Führerscheinbehörde alle notwendigen Unterlagen vorliegen und alle Gebühren entrichtet wurden"*). With an eight-week queue, order the translation the day you book the appointment. Note: *„Klassifizierung"* appears nowhere on Berlin's checklist — it is ADAC's framing, and authorities may or may not want it.

**No apostille or legalisation is required.** Not on Berlin's checklist and not required by §§ 29–31 FeV. Do not pay for one unless LABO asks.

### 2. One appointment, three checklist items
Book a **9 × 45 min first-aid course that includes the eye test and paper biometric photos**. Erste Hilfe Station runs it in **English** and in **Spanish** at Berliner Stadtmission, Frankfurter Allee 96, 10247 Berlin (backyard, entrance via Müggelstraße) — advertised at **59,99 €** *"including free eye test & free photos"*, English courses Saturday/Sunday/Wednesday; bring glasses or contact lenses for the eye test and ID. ⚠️ These are vendor self-claims (the site says *"All offers are not-binding and noncommittal"*) and the claimed Senate certification was not independently checked against a register. What the law requires is § 19 FeV (recognised provider) and § 12 Abs. 2 FeV (amtlich anerkannte Sehteststelle) — verify the provider holds both.

⚠️ Stadt Köln accepts only in-person first-aid courses (*„Wir akzeptieren grundsätzlich nur Nachweise über Erste-Hilfe-Schulungen, die in Präsenz durchgeführt worden sind"*). Berlin's page is silent — **confirm before paying for an online course**.

### 3. Choose the Fahrschule AND the Prüfstelle — before the appointment
The form requires *„Name des Inhabers und Anschrift der Fahrschule sowie Name und Anschrift der Prüfstelle"*, and Berlin warns: *„Nach Erhalt der Prüfungszulassung ist ein Wechsel der Prüfstelle nicht mehr möglich."* So decide three things now:

- **Which Prüfstelle** (DEKRA or TÜV; both operate in Berlin, same fee) — and **ask it whether it can give you a Spanish/English theory slot**, because you cannot change it later.
- **Which Fahrschule** — English or Spanish instruction is worth paying for. Candidates found in Friedrichshain-Kreuzberg: Asphalt Profis (Am Friedrichshain 23, the only Berlin school with a dedicated non-EU conversion page; theory and practical in English — but its published authority fees of 35,00/42,60 € are **outdated**, use it for teaching, not legal facts), 2Drive Perfect (Neue Bahnhofstraße 5, Friedrichshain), Nice Fahrschule (Samariterstraße 40, ~300 m from the same U5 stop as Bürgeramt 3), Fahrschule Kotti (Skalitzer Str. 139, **Spanish**), Fahrschule Nuray (Boppstraße 9, English). ⚠️ Everything except Asphalt Profis is directory-sourced (clickclickdrive.de); confirm languages and Umschreibung handling directly. No Berlin school publishes a fixed conversion package price.
- **Manual or automatic — settle this now.** § 17a Abs. 1 Satz 1 FeV: *„Wird die Prüfungsfahrt auf einem Kraftfahrzeug mit Automatikgetriebe durchgeführt, ist die Fahrerlaubnis auf das Führen von Kraftfahrzeugen mit Automatikgetriebe zu beschränken."* **§ 17a is not switched off by § 31 Abs. 2.** Taking the cheaper, easier automatic exam therefore yields a German class B restricted to automatics (code 78). Escaping that afterwards needs Schlüsselzahl 197, which under § 17a Abs. 3–4 requires a Bescheinigung nach Anlage 7 FahrschAusbO — real, paid manual training, entered within one year — or a further practical exam in a manual under Abs. 2. Berlin also notes that B197 can only be applied for online in an Ersterteilung, and that code-78 holders can have 197 entered only *„im Rahmen eines Umtausches"*. Conversely, § 31 Abs. 1 Satz 5 FeV: if your Argentine licence is itself restricted to vehicles without a clutch pedal, the German licence must be restricted to match. Changing the Prüfauftrag from Schaltung to Getriebeautomatik later is a separate procedure.

### 4. Book the Bürgeramt appointment — Berlin-wide
File at a **Bürgeramt**. The LABO FAQ says *„Anträge auf Umschreibung sind ausschließlich im Bürgeramt zu stellen"* (last updated 03.09.2025), while service.berlin.de says *„Der Antrag kann bei jeder der nachfolgend genannten Behörden gestellt werden"* and lists 59 bookable locations — 58 Bürgerämter **plus** the LABO Fahrerlaubnisbehörde, Puttkamerstr. 16–18. ⚠️ These are two official Berlin sources in conflict; booking a **Bürgeramt satisfies both readings**. If only a LABO slot is offered, confirm via 115 first.

**There is no district restriction:** *„Sie können diese Dienstleistung auch berlinweit in Anspruch nehmen."* Use the Berlin-wide calendar (the *Termin buchen* button on the 327537 page) or phone **(030) 115**, which can book on your behalf and offers some English support. Availability varies sharply between offices — outer districts (Spandau, Marzahn, Reinickendorf, Märkisches Viertel, Bohnsdorf) are usually much better than the central ones. **Booking city-wide is the cheapest available speed-up. Do not wait for a slot in your own district.**

U5 Samariterstraße 0,2 km; U5 Frankfurter Tor 0,5 km; S+U Frankfurter Allee 0,9 km; Tram 21, M10. Phone (030) 115. Appointment mandatory at all times. A no-show without adequate excuse likewise costs the full fee (DEKRA requires a sick note within five days as a rule).

§ 17 Abs. 3 FeV: the practical exam is taken at your place of main residence — i.e. **in Berlin**.

### 8. The second in-person appointment — LABO handover
Most process maps miss this. **There are two appointments, not one.** After passing the practical you hold a Prüfbescheinigung, which is not a driving entitlement. Book an appointment with the **Fahrerlaubnisbehörde (LABO), Puttkamerstr. 16–18, 10969 Berlin**, on **(030) 90269-2300** or via the berlin.de contact form (Mon–Thu 07:00–14:00, Fri 07:00–12:00, appointment-only, no walk-ins). Bring the **original Prüfbescheinigung** and the **original Argentine licence**. You surrender the Argentine licence and receive the **vorläufige Fahrberechtigung**.

LABO is unambiguous that this is not a Bürgeramt matter: for handover after an EU/EEA or Drittstaat Umschreibung *„Zuständig ist **ausschließlich die Fahrerlaubnisbehörde**"*, and *„Der Kartenführerschein wird Ihnen in der Regel anschließend innerhalb von 10 Werktagen postalisch an Ihre Meldeadresse zugestellt."* (Generic Berlin text saying "any Bürgeramt can hand over a licence" does **not** govern this service.)

## Taking the exams

### What is different on this path — and what is not

**Nothing about the exams themselves is different.** There is no "Umschreibungsprüfung" in German law. Anlage 7 FeV has exactly two theory tables — *Ersterwerb* and *Erweiterung* — and *Erweiterung* means adding a class to an **existing German** Fahrerlaubnis, which you do not have. So the full **Ersterwerb** class B exam applies, and the practical is the full-length class B exam with no conversion discount.

What *is* different: no mandatory training, no mandatory lessons, no Ausbildungsbescheinigung, and — usefully — **no annotation on your German card**. § 31 Abs. 4 Satz 1 FeV imposes the *"converted from a non-EU licence"* Vermerk only on a licence issued *„auf Grund des Absatzes 1 Satz 1"*, i.e. Anlage-11 exam-free conversions. Your grant runs under **Abs. 2**, so no Vermerk is required and the card reads as an ordinary German first grant. That is a real advantage if you later move to another EU state, because it avoids the § 28 Abs. 4 Nr. 7 problem entirely. (⚠️ This changes under Directive (EU) 2025/2205 Art. 15(2)–(3), which will require every third-country exchange to carry an Annex I Part E code from ~2029.)

### Theory exam

| Parameter | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | **30** | Anlage 7 Nr. 1.2.2, Ersterwerb (AM/A1/A2/A/B/L/T) |
| Total point pool | **110** | same |
| Maximum error points | **10** | same |
| Automatic fail | **two questions of weight 5 answered wrongly**, regardless of the error-point total | Anlage 7 Nr. 1.2.2 footnote + Nr. 1.2.3 |
| Question weights | 2 to 5 points | Anlage 7 |
| Composition | 20 Grundstoff + 10 Zusatzstoff | Prüfungsrichtlinie / Fragenkatalog — **not** the FeV; Anlage 7 Nr. 1.2.1 only requires questions from both areas |
| Retake | in full, after ≥ ~2 weeks | § 18 Abs. 1 FeV |

**Languages — a statutory entitlement, not a courtesy.** Anlage 7 Nr. 1.3 FeV sets German as the default and then, *„Abweichend von Satz 1"*, permits exactly **twelve** foreign languages: **Englisch, Französisch, Griechisch, Italienisch, Polnisch, Portugiesisch, Rumänisch, Russisch, Kroatisch, Spanisch, Türkisch, Hocharabisch**. Spanish, Italian and English are all available, all at the same fee (DEKRA prices the item as *"Theorieprüfung (Gruppenprüfung) in Deutsch oder Fremdsprachen"*). If a Prüfstelle hesitates, cite Anlage 7 Nr. 1.3 FeV via § 16 Abs. 2 FeV.

Two limits nobody mentions:
- **Audio support over headphones is legally German-only.** Sitting the exam in Spanish gets you Spanish *questions* but no Spanish audio.
- **The only interpreter admitted is a sign-language interpreter for deaf candidates.** Anlage 7 provides no general interpreter, and a VG Berlin ruling (reported 2011) confirmed no interpreter in the theory exam — the twelve translations are the accommodation.

⚠️ Kurmandschi and Ukrainisch are legislated to join from **1 April 2027** (BR-Drs. 318/26 replacing Anlage 7 Nr. 1.3 Satz 4 lit. l and adding a new § 76a FeV) — **not yet in force**.

**Use a current question catalogue.** The catalogue is updated twice yearly, on **1 April** and **1 October**. As of August 2026 the version in force dates from 01.04.2026. The English and Spanish versions are translations of the German catalogue and read as bureaucratic — ⚠️ this is secondary-source advice, but the practical point stands: **study in the language you will sit the exam in**, so the phrasing is familiar.

For scale: 2,04 million theory exams were taken in 2025, with an overall failure rate of 41 % (TÜV-Verband, published 11.02.2026). ⚠️ A separate figure of "~1,7 million theory exams annually" also appears in the research, attributed to TÜV|DEKRA arge tp 21; the two conflict, so use the TÜV-Verband figure.

| Grundfahraufgaben | **exactly three** | Anlage 7 Nr. 2.1.4.2 |
| Abfahrtkontrolle | **none** for class B | Anlage 7 Nr. 2.1.2 (C/C1/D/D1/T only) |

| Structure | 2.1.1 fahrtechnische Vorbereitung → 2.1.4 Grundfahraufgaben → 2.1.5 Prüfungsfahrt → 2.1.6 fahrtechnischer Abschluss, assessed as **one single Prüfungsteil** | Anlage 7 Nr. 2.5.1 — nothing can be part-passed and carried over on a class B retake |
| Failure | one immediate-termination error, **or** *„die Wiederholung oder Häufung von leichten oder schweren Fehlern"* | Anlage 7 Nr. 2.5.2 |

**The three Grundfahraufgaben:** one compulsory choice from group (a) — *„Fahren nach rechts rückwärts unter Ausnutzung einer Einmündung, Kreuzung oder Einfahrt"* **or** *„Rückwärtsfahren in eine Parklücke (Längsaufstellung)"* — plus **two** from group (b): *„Umkehren"*, *„Einfahren in eine Parklücke (Quer- oder Schrägaufstellung)"*, *„Abbremsen mit höchstmöglicher Verzögerung"*. So one reversing task is guaranteed and the examiner picks two more. Hard criteria from the official Fahraufgabenkatalog (Verkehrsblatt-Dokument Nr. S 3210, Vers. 01|21): reverse right around a corner *„in einem möglichst engen Bogen"*, stop parallel to the kerb, **maximum two Korrekturzüge**, no mounting the kerb; parallel park into an ~8 m gap, final distance to the kerb **not more than 30 cm**, max two Korrekturzüge; bay/angle park with the outline inside the marked bay, max two Korrekturzüge; *Umkehren* — you choose the place and method and must observe the Rechtsfahrgebot; emergency braking *„aus mindestens 30 km/h mit höchstmöglicher Verzögerung zum Stillstand"*, *„schlagartiges Betätigen der Betriebsbremse"*, hold your line, do not stall. **The emergency-braking instruction comes from the Fahrlehrer, not the examiner**, and rear observation before it is expressly not required there.

⚠️ Several driving-school pages describe a 5–10 minute "Sicherheitskontrolle" or technical questioning before moving off for class B. That is **not** an Abfahrtkontrolle under Nr. 2.1.2; it belongs to 2.1.1 (seat, mirrors, belt, head restraint, steering-wheel adjustment) and may include a question or two about oil level, tyre pressure, warning lamps or wiper/light controls. Exact scope: **medium confidence — ask your Fahrschule.**

**The single biggest gap for you:** because you are exempt from Sonderfahrten, you may never have driven a German Autobahn — yet the exam route is *supposed* to include one. **Autobahn practice is built into the exam, not optional advice.** Do at least one Autobahn lesson.

2025 figures: 1,89 million practical exams, overall failure rate 31 % (up 1 point), **37 % for class B/BF17** (TÜV-Verband).

⚠️ Also note § 18 Abs. 3 FeV: the examiner must notify the authority if he observes facts raising doubts about your physical or mental fitness. The exam is a fitness-screening event too.

Anlage 7's language provision sits inside **Teil 1 (Theoretische Prüfung)** and has **no counterpart in Teil 2**. There is no statutory language rule for the practical exam and no interpreter provision — German is administrative practice, universally reported.

**This is where your language risk actually sits**, not on the theory exam. Three mitigations: an English- or Spanish-speaking Fahrlehrer, learning the standard examiner phrases below, and choosing a Prüfstelle experienced with foreign candidates. ⚠️ Whether any accommodation is possible in the practical exam is **unverified** — worth asking the Berlin Prüfstelle directly.

### The failure habits of experienced foreign drivers

Each of the first four below is marked with an asterisk in the official Fahraufgabenkatalog, where the legend reads *„Schwere Fehler (\* = sofortige Beendigung)"* — **immediate termination of the exam**.

1. **Rechts vor links.** StVO § 8 Abs. 1 Satz 1: „An Kreuzungen und Einmündungen hat die Vorfahrt, wer von rechts kommt." *„Nichtbeachten der Vorfahrt- oder Vorrangregelung\*"* ends the exam; *„Unzureichende Beachtung"* (the priority vehicle has to slow) is a schwerer Fehler. **The highest-consequence rule for a driver from a country where unmarked junctions are handled by convention.** Berlin's residential side streets are full of unsigned junctions. The catalogue also requires you to *recognise* the regulation on approach and slow down appropriately — approaching too fast is scored **even if no conflict arises**. Also asterisked: *„Nichtbeachten von Vorschriftzeichen\*"* and *„Nichtbeachten von Lichtzeichenanlagen\*"* (running a red light).
2. **Verkehrsbeobachtung / Schulterblick.** *„Fehlende Verkehrsbeobachtung\*"* ends the exam; *„flüchtige seitliche und/oder rückwärtige Verkehrsbeobachtung"* is a schwerer Fehler. The catalogue expects rear and lateral observation, and where relevant a blind-spot check, before **moving off, merging, changing lanes, overtaking, re-entering the lane, turning and entering a roundabout**. **The head movement must be visible to the examiner — a glance he cannot see counts as not done.** German driving-school commentary ranks this the number-one failure cause. Statutory basis: StVO § 9 Abs. 1 Satz 4 — „Vor dem Einordnen und nochmals vor dem Abbiegen ist auf den nachfolgenden Verkehr zu achten; **vor dem Abbiegen ist es dann nicht nötig, wenn eine Gefährdung nachfolgenden Verkehrs ausgeschlossen ist.**" So the second observation is not absolutely unconditional — but in an exam, do it every time.
3. **Speed, measured in single km/h.** *„Überschreitung der zulässigen Höchstgeschwindigkeit ohne zeitnahe Korrektur\*"*, with the indicator *„i.g.O. mehr als 5 km/h und a.g.O. mehr als 10 km/h zu schnell … Eine zeitnahe Korrektur liegt vor, wenn eine erforderliche Geschwindigkeitsanpassung innerhalb von 3 Sekunden und in einer verkehrsüblichen Weise erfolgt."* More than **5 km/h** over the limit in town (10 outside) **ends the exam** unless corrected within three seconds; correcting in time still scores a schwerer Fehler. Inner Berlin is dominated by Tempo-30 zones with frequent changes and time-limited school zones. **Driving too slowly is also an error** (*„übertrieben zögerlich"*, obstructing traffic). Following distance is numeric too: closer than **half** the speedometer reading in metres without timely correction is a schwerer Fehler; closer than a **quarter** is worse.
4. **Cyclists.** StVO § 5 Abs. 4 Satz 3: minimum lateral clearance when overtaking pedestrians, cyclists and e-scooter riders is **1,5 m in town, 2 m outside**. The catalogue operationalises normal clearance as ≥1 m at 50 km/h, +10 cm per 10 km/h, with single-track vehicles and pedestrians always ≥1,5 m; falling below is a schwerer Fehler. Two traps for foreign drivers: (a) **StVO § 5 Abs. 8 lets cyclists legally filter up your right side while you wait at a light** — which is exactly why the shoulder check before turning right is non-negotiable in Berlin; (b) **StVO § 9 Abs. 3 Satz 1** obliges you when turning to let through not only oncoming traffic but also cyclists, mopeds and e-scooters *„auch dann, wenn sie auf oder neben der Fahrbahn in der gleichen Richtung fahren"*, and to wait for pedestrians if necessary. **Berlin has among the highest cycling volumes in Germany — this is the highest-frequency risk of the whole exam.**
5. **Roundabouts are inverted versus Argentine habit.** StVO § 8 Abs. 1a Satz 2: „Bei der Einfahrt in einen solchen Kreisverkehr ist die Benutzung des Fahrtrichtungsanzeigers **unzulässig**." **Do not indicate on entry; you must indicate before leaving.** Indicating on entry or failing to indicate on exit is a leichter Fehler; driving the wrong way round is asterisked. The catalogue also expects you to check the indicator has self-cancelled, to indicate before a lane change inside a multi-lane roundabout — and **not to be over-cautious**: *„Übertrieben zögerliches Heranfahren an einen Kreisverkehr"* is a listed error, as is stopping before entry when a vehicle from the left has clearly slowed and signalled that it is exiting.
6. **Trams.** Fahraufgabe 6.2 is dedicated to approaching trams and tram rails. Positioning: *„Sollte ein Überholen der Straßenbahn möglich sein, erfolgt dies in der Regel **rechts**. Links überholt werden darf in Einbahnstraßen oder wenn die Schienen zu weit rechts liegen."* Asterisked errors here include failing to observe the tram or the rail course, and forcing a priority tram to brake sharply. StVO § 20 Abs. 2: where passengers are boarding or alighting you may pass on the right **only at walking pace** and at a distance that excludes endangering them — *„Wenn nötig, muss, wer ein Fahrzeug führt, warten."* § 20 Abs. 4 extends walking pace to **oncoming traffic on the same carriageway** at bus stops where a service bus or marked school bus has hazard lights on. Fahraufgabe 7.1 sets ≥2 m lateral distance to a bus or tram stopped at a stop and scores *„Vermeidbares Versperren des Ein- und Aussteigens"* as a schwerer Fehler. **Much of eastern Berlin is served by trams (M10, M13, 21, M21 and others) with kerbside stops.**
7. **Accumulated sloppiness fails you just as surely.** Anlage 7 Nr. 2.5.2 fails the exam on *„die Wiederholung oder Häufung von leichten oder schweren Fehlern"*. The catalogue's leichte Fehler include *„Ruckartiges Bremsen"*, *„Keine zweckmäßige Lenkradhaltung"*, *„'Eckige' Lenkbewegungen"*, *„Abwürgen des Motors"*, *„Geringfügiges Zurückrollen des Fahrzeugs [weniger als ca. 50 cm]"* and fumbling the wipers or lights. Environmentally-conscious driving (early upshifting, coasting) is its own scored category. **Any physical intervention by the examiner or instructor is an automatic fail.**
8. ⚠️ Other frequently-cited causes (secondary sources, medium confidence), roughly by frequency: mirror/shoulder checks before turning or lane changes; right-of-way errors; unsuitable speed in both directions; poor lane discipline / Rechtsfahrgebot; hill starts (rolling back, stalling, jerky clutch); distraction (looking at navigation); hesitant motorway driving; sloppy parking.

## The Italian-passport question

You will inevitably ask whether you can convert the Argentine licence in Italy — where it *is* convertible without exams — and then drive in Germany on the Italian *patente*. **You cannot. Today the answer is a flat no, and even after the amendment described below it stays no for you.**

### What is true about the Italian side

- **Argentina genuinely is on Italy's exam-free conversion list.** Italy has reciprocity agreements with roughly 34 non-EU/EEA states permitting conversion without theory or practical exam ([mit.gov.it](https://www.mit.gov.it/conversione-patente-estera); [Il Portale dell'Automobilista, ARGENTINA annex](https://www.ilportaledellautomobilista.it/gms/dettaglionormativa.downloadallegato_0:download/113437/ARGENTINA.pdf)).
- **Argentina sits in Italy's four-year deadline group** — *„4 anni (Albania, Argentina, Svizzera e Ucraina)"* from acquiring Italian residence; past that the application is rejected outright, with no revision-exam fallback.
- **Cost and form:** 42,20 € in state fees (10,20 € + 32 € via PagoPA) on form **TT2112** at a Motorizzazione Civile office. ⚠️ The ~30–60 working-day timeline is an unverified estimate, and the document list in the original research was wrong (it listed a *permesso di soggiorno*, which an Italian citizen does not have).
- **Italian residence is a precondition, and it must precede nothing.** MIT requires *„una residenza anagrafica o una residenza normale in Italia"* **and** that the foreign licence be still valid **and** *„conseguita prima di acquisire la residenza in Italia"*. ⚠️ The further claim that AIRE registration (i.e. registration as an Italian citizen resident abroad) disqualifies you from obtaining or renewing an Italian licence under Art. 118-bis Codice della Strada, per MIT circolare 18710 of 01.07.2024, **could not be verified**. It is legally plausible and directionally consistent with the residence requirement — treat it as unconfirmed but likely.
- ⚠️ **Whether Italy currently converts Argentine licences exam-free could not be re-verified in the final research pass** (mit.gov.it conversion pages returned 404). It does not change the German answer, but do not present the Italian side as certain.

### ⚠️ Why it is legally closed in Germany

> **§ 28 Abs. 4 Satz 1 Nr. 7 FeV** withdraws the German driving entitlement from holders of an EU/EEA licence *„deren Fahrerlaubnis aufgrund einer Fahrerlaubnis eines Drittstaates, der nicht in der Anlage 11 aufgeführt ist, prüfungsfrei umgetauscht worden ist, oder deren Fahrerlaubnis aufgrund eines gefälschten Führerscheins eines Drittstaates erteilt wurde."*

Argentina is exactly such a state. So an Italian licence obtained by exam-free conversion of your Argentine licence confers **no right to drive in Germany**, and using it here would still be Fahren ohne Fahrerlaubnis under § 21 StVG.

Two further, independent bars:

- **§ 28 Abs. 4 Satz 1 Nr. 8 FeV** denies the entitlement to holders who *„zum Zeitpunkt der Erteilung einer Fahrerlaubnis eines Drittstaates, die in eine ausländische EU- oder EWR-Fahrerlaubnis umgetauscht worden ist, oder zum Zeitpunkt der Erteilung der EU- oder EWR-Fahrerlaubnis auf Grund einer Fahrerlaubnis eines Drittstaates ihren Wohnsitz im Inland hatten."* That is **precisely** the Argentina-to-Italy-while-living-in-Berlin constellation.
- **§ 28 Abs. 4 Satz 1 Nr. 2 FeV** independently bars any EU licence issued while your ordinary residence was in Germany, *„es sei denn, dass sie als Studierende oder Schüler im Sinne des § 7 Absatz 2 die Fahrerlaubnis während eines mindestens sechsmonatigen Aufenthalts erworben haben."*

**There is no cure.** The restoration procedure in § 28 Abs. 5 FeV reaches only Abs. 4 Nr. 3 and Nr. 4 (withdrawal / court refusal). It cannot fix a Nr. 7 or Nr. 8 defect.

**EU law expressly permits this.** Art. 11(6) of Directive 2006/126/EC: *"If the holder of this licence transfers his normal residence to another Member State, the latter need not apply the principle of mutual recognition set out in Article 2."* Art. 15(2) of the successor Directive (EU) 2025/2205 keeps the same rule, and Art. 15(5) makes surrender mandatory. So this is not a German quirk that free movement defeats.

**And Italy could not lawfully issue while your Hauptwohnsitz is Berlin.** Italy requires residenza anagrafica or residenza normale in Italy; § 7 FeV sets ordinary residence at 185 days plus personal/occupational ties. Obtaining an Italian licence while actually living in Berlin would be invalid on both sides — Italy may not issue it, Germany would not recognise it — and driving on it here would expose you to prosecution. **This is licence tourism. Do not attempt it.**

### ⚠️ The one pending change — and why it still does not help you

**BR-Drs. 318/26** (28.05.2026), Artikel 2 Nummer 5, **deletes the first alternative of § 28 Abs. 4 Satz 1 Nr. 7**. The official Begründung: the rule *„wurde insgesamt eingeführt, um den Führerscheintourismus zu bekämpfen … Da die Regelung Alternative 1 nun vermehrt ein Hindernis für die Freizügigkeit in der Europäischen Union darstellt, wird sie gestrichen."* ADAC reports the same: *„Außerdem sollen künftig auch Führerscheine aus Drittstaaten, die bereits in einem anderen EU-Staat umgetauscht wurden, in Deutschland anerkannt werden."*

Status: Bundesrat consent **10 July 2026** (Drucksache 318/26(Beschluss)); in force the day after promulgation in the Bundesgesetzblatt; **not yet in the BGBl. as of the index date 31.07.2026** (latest indexed: BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 229), and both gesetze-im-internet.de and buzer.de still showed Nr. 7 intact on 2026-08-04. ⚠️ The exact session reference (1067. Sitzung, TOP 61) could not be independently confirmed in one verification pass — treat the citation, not the substance, as unverified.

**Why it still does not open the Italy route for you:** BR-Drs. 318/26 does **not** touch Nr. 8, and Nr. 2 is untouched too. Both key on **your residence at the relevant time**. As a Berlin resident who would convert in Italy, you remain blocked. The repeal would only help someone who genuinely lived in Italy, converted there, and *then* moved to Germany — which would mean actually relocating your life to Italy for at least 185 days a year first.

**The only Italy variant that would ever produce a Germany-valid licence** is passing the **Italian** theory and practical exams while genuinely resident in Italy (§ 28 Abs. 1 FeV then applies normally). That is not cheaper or faster than the Berlin route; it is a relocation.

### What the Italian passport *does* do for you

- It removes the *„Aufenthaltstitel, soweit Sie nicht EU/EWR-Bürger sind"* line from the Berlin checklist entirely. No residence permit exists or is required; the Italian passport (or carta d'identità) is your ID document.
- It makes the Anmeldung and the whole file straightforward.
- Italian is one of the twelve permitted theory-exam languages — though Spanish is equally available, so pick whichever you read most fluently.

**That is all.** §§ 28–31 FeV attach to **the state that issued the licence**, not to the holder's nationality. Do not let anyone — including a driving school — tell you the Italian passport shortens the process.

## Probezeit and life after

### Probation attaches — and is then credited away

> **§ 2a Abs. 1 Satz 1–2 StVG, verbatim:** „Bei erstmaligem Erwerb einer Fahrerlaubnis wird diese auf Probe erteilt; die Probezeit dauert zwei Jahre vom Zeitpunkt der Erteilung an. Bei Erteilung einer Fahrerlaubnis an den Inhaber einer im Ausland erteilten Fahrerlaubnis ist die Zeit seit deren Erwerb auf die Probezeit anzurechnen."

The honest answer is neither a plain yes nor a plain no: probation **attaches in principle**, then is **consumed by credit** for the time you have held the Argentine licence. **The statute contains no cap on the creditable time** (confirmed verbatim on gesetze-im-internet.de, dejure.org and buzer.de). If you have held the Argentine licence for more than two years, the Probezeit is fully exhausted at the moment of issue and the § 2a Abs. 2 escalation ladder (Aufbauseminar, verkehrspsychologische Beratung, withdrawal) never bites.

There is corroboration from the fee side: Berlin's 45,10 € is built on GebOSt **Nr. 126.2** („in den übrigen Fällen", 1,00 €) rather than **Nr. 126.1** („bei Fahrerlaubnissen auf Probe", 1,80 €).

⚠️ **Unresolved conflict.** One secondary source (ERGO Rechtsportal) claims that for third-country licences the credit is limited to the period from acquisition up to German residence **plus at most six months**. The statute grants credit for *„die Zeit seit deren Erwerb"* without limitation and ERGO's framing is unsupported by any text found. **Get LABO to confirm in writing** whether your German licence is issued auf Probe and, if so, how much Probezeit remains.

Two further § 2a mechanics, for completeness: Satz 5 suspends the running of the Probezeit during Beschlagnahme/Sicherstellung/Verwahrung under § 94 StPO, provisional withdrawal under § 111a StPO, and immediately enforceable withdrawal; Satz 6–7 end it early on withdrawal or renunciation, with only the residual duration reviving on a new licence.

### You will lose the Argentine licence

> **§ 31 Abs. 4 FeV, verbatim (relevant sentences):** „Der auf Grund des Absatzes 1 oder 2 ausgestellte Führerschein ist nur gegen Abgabe des ausländischen Führerscheins auszuhändigen. Die Fahrerlaubnisbehörde sendet ihn über das Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt an die Stelle zurück, die ihn ausgestellt hat, wenn mit dem betreffenden Staat eine entsprechende Vereinbarung besteht. In den anderen Fällen nimmt sie den Führerschein in Verwahrung. Er darf nur gegen Abgabe des auf seiner Grundlage ausgestellten inländischen Führerscheins wieder ausgehändigt werden. **In begründeten Fällen kann die Fahrerlaubnisbehörde davon absehen**, den ausländischen Führerschein in Verwahrung zu nehmen oder ihn an die ausländische Stelle zurückzuschicken. Verwahrte Führerscheine können nach drei Jahren vernichtet werden."

LABO confirms: *„Bei allen Umschreibungen gilt, dass der ausländische Führerschein bei der Aushändigung des deutschen Führerscheins eingezogen und ins Ausstellungsland zurückgeschickt oder aufbewahrt wird."* Surrender is also EU-mandated (Art. 11(6) Dir 2006/126/EC; Art. 15(5) Dir (EU) 2025/2205).

**Assume you will lose physical possession.** Before you hand it in: make high-resolution scans and certified copies, and obtain the ANSV Certificado de Legalidad (free) — you will need these for any future dealings with Argentine authorities.

⚠️ **Whether a Germany–Argentina return agreement exists under § 31 Abs. 4 Satz 3 FeV is unverified.** Germany has no Anlage-11 arrangement with Argentina, which makes a return agreement unlikely, so the default of Verwahrung in Berlin (destructible after three years) is the working assumption. **Ask LABO directly, in these words:** *„Wird mein argentinischer Führerschein nach Argentinien zurückgeschickt oder hier in Verwahrung genommen? Kann ich ihn ungültig gestempelt zurückerhalten (§ 31 Abs. 4 Satz 6 FeV)?"*

**A specific Argentine complication.** Since **Decreto 196/2025** (Boletín Oficial 18 March 2025, reported effective 19 May 2025) the Argentine licence is issued **digital by default** — *„la licencia nacional de conducir será otorgada en formato digital"*, which *„podrá ser replicada en formato físico"*, and *„Ambas tienen la misma validez legal"*. But § 31 Abs. 4 FeV requires a **physical document to surrender**, and Italy likewise demands *"patente originale e fotocopia"*. **A digital-only licence gives you nothing to hand over. Secure a valid physical card before you apply**, and raise the § 31 Abs. 4 Satz 6 discretion with LABO in advance.

### Driving in Argentina afterwards

- **Your German card works as a visitor.** Argentina recognises national licences from parties to the 1949 Geneva or 1968 Vienna conventions for *„el período máximo de un año desde su ingreso"* — a maximum of one year from entry. Germany is a party to both. Carry passport plus licence. An international driving permit is valid one year from issue and becomes necessary only *„si no se exige en su propio país una licencia de conducir"* — so it is **optional** for a German licence holder, but cheap belt-and-braces against a roadside language problem. ⚠️ Germany's contracting-party status under the 1949 Geneva Convention could not be independently re-verified in the final pass.
- **This addresses visitors.** If you were ever treated as resident in Argentina again, an Argentine licence would be expected.
- **Renewing the Argentine licence requires Argentina.** Renewal of the Licencia Nacional de Conducir needs a DNI showing a **current Argentine address**, an in-person appointment at the Centro Emisor of that municipality, a psychophysical examination and payment of the CeNAT. Standard validity five years. **No remote or from-abroad procedure is documented.**
- **The Berlin consulate cannot help.** Argentine diplomatic and consular offices *„no tienen ninguna competencia en materia de emisión y/o renovación de licencias de conducir"*, and the Certificado de Legalidad *„debe ser gestionado por el propio interesado ante la ANSV"*. (Consular Section: Von-der-Heydt-Str. 2, 10785 Berlin.) ⚠️ The consulate's published position on licence competence could not be re-fetched (HTTP 403); the address is confirmed.

### Long-term change flags

- **Directive (EU) 2025/2205** (adopted 22 October 2025) repeals Directive 2006/126/EC and, in Art. 15, creates for the first time an **EU-level third-country exchange regime**: Art. 15(6)–(7) empower the Commission to determine that a third country's road-safety framework is comparable to the Union's and to adopt an implementing act, after which Member States exchange those licences on harmonised conditions (Art. 15(3)) — effectively an EU-wide successor to Anlage 11 that could one day cover Argentina. Art. 15(2) preserves the national route and the receiving state's right to refuse; Art. 15(5) makes surrender mandatory. ⚠️ Timing: transposition by **26 November 2028**, application from **26 November 2029** (a subset by 26 November 2027) — these dates are **partly unverified**; only the 22 October 2025 adoption and the repeal of 2006/126/EC were confirmed. **Nothing changes for you before late 2029. There is no reason to wait** — but re-check if your plan slips by years.
- **Anlage 11 is a live list.** BR-Drs. 318/26 adds **Ukraine and Montenegro**, expands Israel's entry from class B to A1/A2/A/B, and restates the Kosovo and Albania rows. **Argentina appears nowhere in it** and there is no indication of any pending Germany–Argentina licence agreement. Note also that Anlage 11 is not uniformly exam-free: Moldau still requires a practical exam, Albania requires one for class AM, several US states require a theory exam, and Anmerkung 7 preserves the § 12 eye-vision proof for others. Anmerkung 1: where the class column names specific classes rather than *„alle"*, *„erfolgt aufgrund dieser Klasse(n) nur die Erteilung der Klasse B."*
- **Do not wait for the 2027 Fahrschulreform.** BR-Drs. 365/26 would cut the class B practical exam from 55/30 to **40/25 minutes** and rescore theory to one point per question with three errors allowed plus instant-fail starred questions — the only two items that touch a conversion candidate. Its headline savings (abolishing compulsory theory lessons and Sonderfahrten) are **worth nothing to you**, because § 31 Abs. 2 FeV already exempts you from all of it. Timing is *„not before 1 January 2027, most likely 1 July 2027, and only once promulgated"* — the Drucksache's own table of contents and Begründung disagree with its operative text about article numbering and dates. Meanwhile your six-month recognition window expires and § 21 StVG is a criminal offence. **Go now.**
- **§ 31 Abs. 1a FeV** (a provision worth knowing about even though it does not apply to you) lets the authority order a full Fahrerlaubnisprüfung for an Anlage-11 applicant *„wenn Tatsachen vorliegen, die die Annahme rechtfertigen, dass der Bewerber die nach § 16 Absatz 1 und § 17 Absatz 1 erforderlichen Kenntnisse und Fähigkeiten nicht mehr besitzt."* It defeats any advice that an Anlage-11 detour would be reliably exam-free — and it means a long-dormant licence cannot be used to argue exams away.

## Open questions to ask the authority

Take this list to your Bürgeramt appointment, and put the starred ones to LABO **in writing** (contact form on berlin.de/labo, or (030) 90269-2300).

**Blocking or expensive if wrong**

11. **Is there a charge for the befristete Prüfungsbescheinigung / vorläufige Fahrberechtigung** (GebOSt Nr. 202.7, 10,20 €, *„soweit vom Bewerber veranlasst"*)?
